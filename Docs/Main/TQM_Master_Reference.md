## 1 Executive Summary

**Core statement:**

> **Structure is derivable. Content is realized.**

TQM compresses observable physics to a small primitive set. The *form* of every
structure (gauge groups, spatial dimensionality, the abundance law, the multiplicity
lower bound) is **DERIVED** by theorem from the primitives. The *content* (specific
masses, couplings, multiplicities, the Koide angle) is **REALIZED** — a draw from a
contingent ensemble — and is **DRAWN** / **REAL-UNDERIVED**, not computable.

**Minimal taxonomy (Phase 158, stress-tested):**

## 3 Core Principles

**Structure / content split.**
Form (topology, symmetry, distribution shape, lower bounds) is derived; content
(drawn values, specific angles, upper bounds) is contingent.

**Universal Abundance Law (XB002).**
Every abundance quantity is log-normally distributed:

$$\log X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$$

Multiplicative actualization cascades ⇒ CLT in log-space. Three universality
classes: coupling, mass scale, relic density.

**Random Actualization (QG-042/064).**
Content is realized by ontological chance, not by a selection principle.

**Complexity principle (X029/XE009).**
Complexity maximization peaks at $M^2 \approx 5 \Rightarrow d = 3+1$.
