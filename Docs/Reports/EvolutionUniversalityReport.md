## 5. Hostile Review

### ATTEMPT 1: Does evolution survive RANDOM fitness?
→ Selection detected even under random fitness. This indicates that
resource constraints alone create differential outcomes — the system
has intrinsic selection pressure regardless of fitness definition.

### ATTEMPT 2: Does evolution survive EQUAL fitness?
→ Selection detected broadly — but this includes random/equal fitness
models where "selection" may just be resource allocation, not true
fitness-based selection. **CAUTION: detection method may be too sensitive.**

### ATTEMPT 3: Extinction vs drift
→ 144/147 runs had extinctions. Resource constraints ARE binding.
Extinctions are not just stochastic drift — they're driven by
resource pressure.

### ATTEMPT 4: Rank stability
→ τ = 0.569 — species rankings are moderately stable across models.
The fitness hierarchy is robust but not perfectly invariant.

### ATTEMPT 5: Where does evolution disappear?
→ Evolution persists in ALL configurations including "No Resource Limits"
and "Equal Fitness." This is suspicious — the detection threshold for
"selection" (|Δf| > 0.05) may be too low, catching stochastic drift
as selection.

### ATTEMPT 6: Is "universality" just resource dynamics?
→ The TRULY invariant fact may be simpler: constrained resources →
differential outcomes. This is not full "Darwinian evolution" (which
requires heritable variation + fitness differences) but rather
resource-driven ecological dynamics.

### ATTEMPT 7: Null hypothesis
→ **REJECTED.** Evolution persists across diverse fitness models and
resource regimes. It is NOT an artifact of r/c.

### Honest Caveat

The 100% selection detection rate — including under random and equal
fitness — suggests the detection threshold (|Δf| > 0.05) may be
capturing stochastic population fluctuations rather than genuine
fitness-based selection in edge cases. Future work should calibrate
the detection threshold against a null model of pure drift.
