# Observable Consequences (QG-094)

If spacetime is fundamentally discrete, what observable traces remain?

## Two suppression scales
1. **Cosmological**: 1/√N ~ 1e-122 (Poisson fluctuations of the causet about the continuum).
2. **Propagation**: l_P/λ ~ 1e-28 (swerving / Lorentz violation at the Planck scale).

## Signal amplitudes vs detector sensitivities
| channel | amplitude | sensitivity | S/N |
|---|---|---|---|
| dark energy Λ | 1 (observed) | 1 | 1 |
| Λ fluctuation | 1e-122 | 1e-4 | ~1e-118 |
| GW phase noise | l_P·f/c ~ 1e-36 | 1e-22 (LIGO) | ~1e-14 |
| photon time-delay | l_P/c ~ 1e-43 s | 1e-6 s (pulsar) | ~1e-37 |
| CMB low-l variance | ~0.7 | 0.1 | O(1) — but NOT unique |
| LSS correlation excess | 1e-122 | 1e-4 | ~1e-118 |

## Verdict
Every Planck-suppressed channel has S/N ≪ 1. The only O(1) entries are Λ (already observed)
and the CMB low-l variance (identical in continuum cosmology — not a unique discreteness
signature). No current or next-generation instrument (JWST, Rubin, Euclid, LISA, ET, CE) can
detect any Planck-suppressed discreteness signature.

**Conclusion:** causal discreteness leaves no observable trace beyond Λ itself. The Λ ~ 1/√N
result is a genuine but untestable postdiction — its only observable consequence is the dark
energy it explains.
