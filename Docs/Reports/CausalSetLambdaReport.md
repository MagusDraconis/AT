# Causal Set Λ Report (QG-093)

Is Λ ~ 1/√N a genuine prediction or numerology?

## The result under scrutiny
- N = (R_H/l_P)⁴ ≈ 5.2×10²⁴³ (elements in the observable Hubble 4-volume).
- Predicted Λ·l_P² = 1/√N ≈ 1.4×10⁻¹²²; observed ≈ 2.9×10⁻¹²².
- Amplitude α = Λ_obs/Λ_pred ≈ 0.48 — O(1), with no free parameter.

## Why it is NOT the next g†-numerology
1. The exponent −1/2 is DERIVED (Sorkin's "ever-present Λ" from spacetime discreteness), not assumed.
2. α = O(1) with zero free parameters (g† needed a tunable 2π factor).
3. Rival exponents (N^-1/4, N^-1/3, N^-2/3, N^-1) are off by ~1e±122 — not by a factor 5.

## Robustness
- Sensitivity d ln Λ / d ln H0 = 2 (Λ ∝ H0²); a 1% H0 error gives 2% in Λ. Insensitive.
- Monte Carlo (H0 ∈ [65,75]) keeps α = O(1) throughout — no narrow tuning.

## Honest caveat (Level 5 fails)
It is a POSTDICTION (explains the magnitude of Λ after the fact), and its fluctuation
~1/√N ≈ 1e-122 is unobservably small. So it has no near-term observational consequence.

## Verdict
Levels 1–4 pass (dimensions, robustness, exponent scan, prediction-vs-numerology); Level 5
fails (no observable). Λ ~ 1/√N is a GENUINE consequence of causal discreteness — the strongest
theoretical result in the QG-080–093 program — but its strength is explanation, not prediction.
