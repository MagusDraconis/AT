# Predictions From Change (QG-090)

What genuinely falsifiable predictions arise from a theory of change?

## The one quantitative prediction: causal-set Λ
Causal Set Theory — where the universe is a growing partially-ordered set of discrete events —
predicts that the discreteness of spacetime at the Planck scale forces the cosmological constant
to have magnitude

    Λ · l_P² ≈ 1/√N ≈ 1e-122   (Planck units),

where N = (R_H/l_P)⁴ ≈ 1e244 is the number of causet elements in the Hubble 4-volume. This
MATCHES the observed dark-energy density (Λ·l_P² ≈ 2.9e-122) to within a factor ~2.

This is the one framework in the QG-080–090 program that is NOT a re-parameterization of FLRW:
it derives the otherwise-inexplicable magnitude of the cosmological constant from the
discreteness of spacetime. Its weakness: the FLUCTUATION around this mean is ~1/√N ≈ 1e-122,
unobservably small — so it explains the value but makes no near-term testable prediction.

## What everything else predicts
- Block universe / Wheeler-DeWitt / thermal time / relational time: observationally equivalent
  to GR+QM in their classical limits — no new prediction (consistent with QG-080–089).
- "It from Bit": information is fundamental, but no clean observable deviation has been derived.
- a₀ = c × (minimum rate of change) = cH (the 'cH class', no 2π) — order-of-magnitude only.

## Verdict
The deeper primitive is the causal partial order, from which time, change, information and
expansion all emerge. Its one quantitative signature is Λ ~ 1/√N. Change is real but emergent;
its measurable consequence (so far) is only the magnitude of Λ.
