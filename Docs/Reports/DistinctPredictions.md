# Distinct Predictions (QG-082)

The search for "beyond conformal time" dynamics comes up empty: γ = a is uniquely forced by
redshift + time dilation. Every alternative family (γ = a^p, γ = a(1+εz), γ = a·e^βz,
γ = a/(1+αz)) reduces to γ = a when its parameter is forced to its FLRW value.

## The only remaining avenue
A genuinely distinct time dynamics could survive **only** if different physical processes
couple to the cosmic clock differently — a drift between clock species:

- gravitational clock (dynamical time, set by G and masses)
- atomic clock (electromagnetic time, set by α and m_e)
- nuclear clock (weak/strong rates)

## What that would look like (the "smoking gun")
1. **Non-universal redshift** — different species (atomic vs. 21-cm vs. molecular) showing
   different z for the same source.
2. **Time-variation of α or G** at the ~H⁻¹ rate.
3. **Clock-rate drift** measurable by precision timing (pulsars, future standard-siren GW
   vs. atomic-clock comparison).

## Observational status
All three are constrained to be consistent with zero (universality of redshift is excellent;
Δα/α and ΔG/G are bounded at ~10⁻⁶..10⁻² per Hubble time). Therefore no beyond-conformal
signal survives.

## Verdict
- Levels 1–3 PASS (alternatives constructed, constraints applied, uniqueness proven).
- Level 4 FAIL — no viable non-FLRW time dynamics.
- Level 5 FAIL — no genuinely distinct prediction survives; the "distinct prediction" that
  WOULD exist (clock-species drift) is observationally forced to zero.

The clock is not encoding FLRW arbitrarily; cosmology forces γ = a. The conformal-time
reinterpretation (QG-080/081) is therefore the *complete* and *unique* time-first description,
and it remains observationally equivalent to ΛCDM.
