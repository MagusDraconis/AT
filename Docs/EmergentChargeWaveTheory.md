# Emergent Charge Wave Theory

## TQM-127: From Discrete Charges to Continuous Field

### Abstract

We show that as charge density increases, the ensemble of Q=+1
topological charges undergoes a particle-to-field crossover.
At low density: independent charges (particle-like). At high
density: coherent wave medium (field-like). This is a classical
emergence phenomenon — continuous field behavior from discrete
topological objects.

### 1. The Particle-Field Crossover

The collective field Θ(x,t) = Σ A_c·exp(iθ_c) behaves differently
in two limits:

**Dilute (N_Q ≪ N_cells)**:
- Charges far apart (d ≫ ξ)
- Each charge's contribution is localized Gaussian
- Θ(x) is a sum of isolated peaks — granular
- No global wave structure

**Dense (N_Q ~ N_cells)**:
- Charges percolate coupling network
- Phases lock globally (R_Q → 1)
- Θ(x) is smooth, continuous — wave-like
- Supports standing and traveling wave modes

### 2. Coherence Length Scaling

ξ(ρ_Q) grows with density:
- ξ ~ λ (coupling range) at low density
- ξ → system size L as ρ_Q → ρ_c
- At percolation: ξ diverges (power law)

### 3. Effective Field Equation

At high density, Θ(x,t) approximately satisfies:

```
∂²Θ/∂t² = v²·∇²Θ − γ·∂Θ/∂t + source terms
```

where v ≈ √(K·λ²/N) is the effective wave velocity and γ is
damping from the reaction-diffusion dynamics.

This is a DAMPED WAVE EQUATION — the charge medium supports
propagating waves but they are damped by the dissipative
Kuramoto-reaction dynamics.

### 4. Structure Factor Evolution

S(k) across densities:
- **Dilute**: S(k) ≈ flat (white noise — no spatial structure)
- **Correlated**: S(k) develops weak peak at k ~ 2π/d_typ
- **Coherent wave**: S(k) has sharp peak at dominant wave number

The emergence of a peak in S(k) is the STRUCTURAL signature
of collective wave formation.

### 5. Conclusion

The charge ensemble exhibits a DENSITY-DRIVEN CROSSOVER from
particle-like to field-like behavior. At low density: discrete
Q=+1 charges with pairwise interactions. At high density:
continuous wave medium with macroscopic coherence. This is
classical emergence — no new physics, just the collective
effect of many interacting topological charge quanta.
