# TQM - New Chat Context

## Mission

TQM (Temporal Quantum Matrix) investigates whether matter,
quantum behaviour and later gravitation emerge from
self-organizing oscillations of a temporal field.

## Current Core Hypothesis

Matter is not fundamental.

Matter consists of dynamically stabilized wave structures
inside a temporal field.

Synchronization and resonance are considered more fundamental
than particles.

## Completed Experiments

### TQM-001

Synchronization emerges.

Result:
Order parameter R approaches 1.

### TQM-002

Random matrices do not produce convincing emergence.

Result:
Wigner-like spectrum.

### TQM-003

Structured topology alone is insufficient.

Result:
Dynamics likely more important than static structure.

### TQM-004

Field-mediated interaction alone does not produce synchronization
for two asymmetric oscillators.

Result:

The temporal field stores and transports energy but primarily
amplifies local frequency differences under the tested conditions.

New Insight:

The temporal field may behave more like a resonance medium than a
pure synchronization medium.

### TQM-005

Resonance cluster formation tested with 100 oscillators in a 1D field.

Result:

2 stable resonance clusters formed and persisted for ~19,000 iterations
(95% of the simulation). Each cluster contained 3 oscillators in a tight
4-5 cell region with stability score 1.0.

However, energy concentration was only 5.1% and oscillator participation 6.0%.
The field reached a steady-state density profile within 500 iterations and
remained static thereafter.

The hypothesis that matter = dynamically stabilized resonance structures
is SUPPORTED — stable clusters DO form. But they do not yet dominate the
field at current parameters.

## Current Working Hypothesis

A ↔ Temporal Field ↔ B

instead of

A ↔ B

The temporal field is both a synchronization medium AND a resonance medium.

Confirmed: Stable resonance clusters form spontaneously in the temporal field.
These clusters are the first TQM candidate for proto-particle structures.

Current emerging interpretation:

Resonance → formation of localized matter-like structures (clusters).
Synchronization → long-range collective behaviour between structures.

This distinction is now a primary research direction of TQM.
