## 3. The Decisive Gap

**Quantum mechanics is not derived from the primitive base.**

- QG61: the Q-event network is classical (discrete ticks + probabilities).
- QG62: complex amplitudes require a phase, which the network does not
  natively have — QM is *compatible* (links can host a U(1) connection) but
  *not emergent*: it **requires a new primitive** (the amplitude/phase).
- QG73: collapse = actualization, but as a **binary** (tick/no-tick)
  projection — a partial match to general measurement.

Gravity, in contrast, is fully derived from ρ. The two pillars of quantum
gravity are **not based on the same primitive**.
