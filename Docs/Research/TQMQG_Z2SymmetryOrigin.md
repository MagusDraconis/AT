## Question

QG153 established that the weak-isospin doublets arise from exact Z2 spectral pairing (reflection and
half-shift automorphisms of the 12-regular adjacency). This phase asks: **why does the observable sector
possess the Z2 symmetry that generates the doublets?**

## Starting Point

- QG153: DOUBLET ORIGIN — 95/95 modes in degenerate Z2 pairs, forced by reflection and half-shift
  automorphisms of the 12-regular adjacency.

## Method

Five deterministic probes:

1. **Reflection symmetry** — seed vs final adjacency invariance under i → n−1−i.
2. **Half-shift symmetry** — seed vs final adjacency invariance under i → i+n/2.
3. **Attractor dynamics** — the symmetry emergence during evolution.
4. **Octave-band structure** — the family bands of the resulting spectrum.
5. **Symmetry selection** — the automorphism group of the converged circulant ring.

All probes are deterministic.

## Assumptions

1. The Z2 symmetries of the adjacency force the eigenvalue degeneracies (QG153).
2. The dynamics create links to cyclic neighbors (i+d) mod n, generating a circulant ring.

## Conclusions

1. The reflection symmetry is **generated by the dynamics** (absent in the seed, present in the
   converged 12-regular adjacency).
2. The half-shift symmetry inherits the period-3 seed structure.
3. Both symmetries emerge as the attractor saturates (step 5) and are stable through step 200.
4. The resulting spectrum carries the 3-family octave structure.
5. The dynamics generate a circulant ring C_n(1..6) with dihedral automorphism group D_n.

## Classification: **SYMMETRY ORIGIN**

- **ACCIDENTAL rejected**: the symmetries are structurally generated (circulant dynamics, period-3 seed)
  and stable across evolution.
- **DYNAMICAL rejected**: the mechanism is stronger than mere emergence — a structural selection exists
  (the circulant ring → dihedral group D_n).
- **SYMMETRY ORIGIN accepted**: the Z2 doublet symmetry is a genuine, dynamically-selected property. The
  attractor generates a circulant ring C_n(1..6) with dihedral symmetry D_n (rotation Z_n + reflection);
  the reflection arises from the dynamics, the half-shift from the period-3 seed, and the 2D irreducible
  representations of D_n generate the Z2 doublets — the symmetry origin of the weak-isospin doublet
  structure.
