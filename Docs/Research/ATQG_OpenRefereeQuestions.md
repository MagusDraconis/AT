## Open Questions

1. **Moment-sector uniqueness:** Is the mapping from `Σ√m`, `Σm`, `Σm²`, and `occMom` to neutrino / charged / doublet / Higgs-cosmology sectors uniquely forced, or only the best-supported labeling?

2. **Operator semantics only:** Can the moment hierarchy be derived from `Crowding`, `Compression`, `Beat`, and `Locking` without any physical labels, or do sector names require a separate phenomenological selection step?

3. **N = 96 scope:** Is `N = 96` inevitable globally, or only within the accepted canonical structural class?

4. **Observable selection risk:** Which observables are natural targets, which are secondary catalog matches, and which remain post-hoc despite structural fit?

5. **Unit origin:** Which observables are genuinely dimensionless outputs, and which only become dimensional after calibration to `m_e`, `v`, or SI conventions?

6. **Hosted dynamics boundary:** Exactly which parts of the Standard Model and cosmological dynamics are reconstructed, and which remain hosted external structure?

7. **Failure modes:** What precisely fails when a moment assignment or calibration anchor is swapped?

## Referee-Safe Status

The current theory supports a strong structural hierarchy and many successful readouts, but it does not yet provide a global uniqueness proof for the sector labels or a complete derivation of all dynamics.
