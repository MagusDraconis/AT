## 4. Is "ψ origin is open" still valid?

**Classification: PARTIALLY SOLVED.**

The ψ origin question has two distinct layers:

1. **Capacity (why links carry a traceless d.o.f.) — SOLVED.**
   QG54 (ψ = Weyl content of causal connectivity) + QG56 (complete link necessarily carries the traceless/Weyl
   d.o.f.; conformal-only links are the restricted case) + QG58 (discrete microscopic, continuous continuum).
   The origin of the Weyl capacity is **forced** by link completeness — not open.

2. **Excitation (why ψ ≠ 0) — DERIVED mechanism, observation-triggered instances.**
   QG57: quadrupole → Weyl sourcing is a consequence of spin-2 coupling to T_μν (derived); which sources
   actually excite it (binaries, mergers) is set by observation.

3. **Derivation from existing primitives — IMPOSSIBLE, and this is structural, not a gap.**
   QG17/19/23/24/52 are explicit and consistent: ψ cannot be derived from or emerge out of the scalar sector.
   It is a genuine new primitive. This is the theory's honest boundary, not an unresolved gap.

**What remains "open" is narrower than the raw claim:**
- ψ is a **NEW PRIMITIVE** — its existence is not derivable from Q-events + counting measure (QG23/24/40/47).
  This is a boundary statement, not an unfinished derivation.
- Its **value/excitation instances** depend on the observation-triggered input (QG57).
- Its **necessity** rests on the model-dependent spin-2 reading of the GW strain (QG48, QG53).
