## Question

QG127 established that high-energy sectors decay through discrete ladders into the
observable 3-family sector. This phase asks: **do the sector transitions generate a
predictable spectrum of emitted energy/information quanta?**

## Starting Point

- QG125: METASTABLE — high sectors decay stepwise down the ladder.
- QG127: OBSERVABLE SIGNATURE — decay leaves a spectrally structured multi-class cascade
  with 8 discrete energy thresholds.

## Method

Within the QG125 de-actualization (link-decay) dynamics, let a high-energy sector decay
under a gradual energy decline (40 ramp steps, 3 evolutions each). Record the discrete
rung radii (the transition ladder). The **emitted energy/information quantum** of each
transition is the magnitude of the radius drop between consecutive rungs. Measure:

1. **Transition ladder spacing** — the rung radii and their spacings.
2. **Emitted-energy analog** — the multiset of emitted quanta (radius drops).
3. **Cascade spectrum** — distinct quantum values with multiplicities (spectral lines).
4. **Threshold structure** — the discrete energy thresholds predicting the ladder.
5. **Observable signatures** — reproducibility across decay speeds and dominance of a
   fundamental quantum.

All probes are deterministic (fixed seed pattern, fixed parameters, no randomness).

## Assumptions

1. The emitted energy/information quantum of a sector transition = |Δradius| between
   consecutive rungs (radius = links per node, an information-density analog).
2. The multiplicity of a quantum value is the strength of its spectral line.
3. A predictive spectrum is DISCRETE, REPRODUCIBLE (independent of decay speed), and
   dominated by a fundamental quantum.
4. The discrete energy thresholds (QG127) predict the transition ladder.

### 2. Emitted-energy analog (ATQG1280)

Each transition emits a quantum equal to the radius drop:

- rung 0→1: **1.333** (the topmost, high→16 transition)
- rungs 1→2 … 10→11: **1.000** each (ten unit-quantum transitions)

### 3. Cascade spectrum (ATQG1281)

The emitted spectrum has **2 lines**:

```
quantum=1.000 × 10   (dominant)
quantum=1.333 ×  1
```

- **Dominant quantum = 1.000**, multiplicity 10, fraction **0.909** (90.9% of all
  emissions).
- Discrete spectrum with a dominant line: **True**.

### 4. Threshold structure (ATQG1281)

The fine ceiling sweep reveals **8 discrete energy thresholds**:

```
1.25 → 1.50 → 1.75 → 2.00 → 2.25 → 2.50 → 2.75 → 3.00
```

These predict the sector-class changes that constitute the transition ladder.

### 5. Observable signatures (ATQG1282)

- **Spectrum reproducible across decay speeds**: True (same rung set and dominant quantum
  for 3 vs 6 evolutions per ramp step).
- **Fundamental unit quantum dominates**: True (Δradius = 1, fraction ≥ 0.5).
- Predictive-spectrum score **5 / 5**.

## Conclusions

1. Sector transitions emit a **discrete, quantized emission ladder** — not a continuous
   slide.
2. The emitted spectrum is dominated by a **fundamental unit quantum** (Δradius = 1 link
   per node), carrying 90.9% of all emissions, with one higher line (Δ = 1.333).
3. The spectrum is **reproducible across decay speeds** — independent of how fast the
   energy declines.
4. The transition ladder is **predicted by 8 discrete energy thresholds**.
