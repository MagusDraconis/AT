# AT-QG Executive One-Pager

**Status:** REFEREE-SAFE SUMMARY FOR FUTURE CHATS, REVIEWS, AND SUBMISSIONS

## What AT claims

AT claims a structurally forced spectrum built from Difference → Actualization → Spectrum, with a fixed operator basis and a forced moment hierarchy. It reconstructs many observables from D96, and it has a compact natural core fixed before observation. Dimensional outputs are obtained only after calibration to three anchors: `m_e`, `v`, and SI conversion conventions. The strongest defensible core claims are the family count, `θ_QCD = 0`, `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, and `M_H`.

## What AT does not claim

AT does not claim global uniqueness for the full observable register. It does not claim that all observables are natural, that sector labels are uniquely forced, or that the SM Lagrangian and interaction dynamics are fully derived. It does not claim complete derivation of the full 29-observable register from first principles without calibration or validation targets.

## Structural core

- D96 as the derived spectral core
- fixed operator basis
- forced moment hierarchy
- explicit calibration anchors
- reconstructed SM observables and cosmology readouts

## Calibration anchors

1. `m_e`
2. `v`
3. SI conversion (`ħ`, `c`, GeV↔kg)

## Natural core

The natural core is the pre-registered subset least exposed to catalog-driven target selection:
- family count
- `θ_QCD = 0`
- blind Higgs reconstruction
- Higgs mass `M_H`
- `P1`
- `P2`
- `P3`
- Majorana character / `m_ββ`

## Remaining open criticisms

- Observable-selection non-uniqueness remains.
- The audited register is mostly secondary (`19/29`) rather than natural (`7/29`).
- Sector-label assignments are supported but not globally unique.
- The strongest remaining criticism is that the observable map is not globally unique.

## Referee-safe bottom line

AT’s strongest case is a compact, pre-registered natural core inside a larger calibrated reconstruction program. Its structural claims are strong; its observable-target uniqueness claims are not.
