## Irreducible Predictive Core of AT

The irreducible predictive core of AT is the subset of natural observables whose targets were fixed before measurement or are structural identities independent of catalog choice. In hostile-referee terms, the core is strongest where the theory either freezes the target in advance or produces an exact invariant; it is weakest where the target is hidden but still calibrated by the same downstream machinery.
