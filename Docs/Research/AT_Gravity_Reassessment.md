## 1. Executive Summary

**counting measure ρ** — to a conformally-flat metric, its curvature, its Einstein tensor, geodesic

But the chain rests on one **load-bearing assumption** — the metric ansatz g = ρ^(2/d)η — and it is
**kinematic, not dynamic**: nothing in the gravity program determines *why ρ has the profile it does*.

so there is **no independent matter sector**; the "matter" is a reinterpretation of ρ itself. The headline
gravity results are therefore: **geometry/curvature/Einstein-structure = DERIVED (high confidence)**;
**matter/Newtonian-gravity/flat-rotation-curves = PARTIALLY DERIVED, resting on hypotheses that are
REAL-UNDERIVED or OPEN**.

   independent field equation with a dynamical source.
6. **Boundary-dominated negative-curvature target** (G4-10): the open Poincaré disk failed the negative-SCI
   target because it is boundary-dominated, not a compact genus-≥2 surface.

## 7. Most Important Unresolved Problems

1. **No dynamical origin of ρ.** The entire gravity program is *kinematic*: it takes ρ(x) as given and
   derives everything from it. Nothing explains *why ρ has the profile it has*, or how it evolves. The actual
   dynamics of the temporal field (the QM/QG programs) are not yet connected to the gravity kinematics.

2. **The metric ansatz g = ρ^(2/d)η is the load-bearing, underived assumption.** Conformally-flat with a
   specific exponent fixed by √g=ρ. This single axiom carries the metric structure; it is not derived from
   dynamics. (If it fails, everything downstream fails.)

3. **No independent matter/energy sector.** Lovelock uniqueness forces T ≡ G/κ, so "matter" is either vacuous
   (G/κ) or a reinterpretation of ρ (the deficit). The QM program's self-stabilizing wave structures are the
   intended matter, but gravity does not (yet) derive them or couple to them.

4. **The exact continuum normalization (BDG scale −2, i.e. Newton's G) is imported.** The native operators
   carry the right signature, causality, and binomial structure, but not the right overall scale.

5. **The selection of the self-similar (α=0) hierarchy for flat rotation curves is a symmetry assumption.**
   It is the unique scale-invariant profile, but *why* actualization is exactly scale-free (α=0 rather than
   any α≠0) is not derived.

## 8. Bottom Line

AT has **derived a coherent, exact kinematic gravity** — geometry, curvature, and the Einstein tensor from
causal order and the counting measure — but it has **not derived dynamics or matter**. Gravity in AT is
currently a *correspondence* (the conformal factor ρ of the counting measure behaves like a metric) rather
than a *field theory* (there are no derived field equations with a dynamical source). The decisive open
questions are (1) the dynamical origin of ρ and (2) whether the QM program's matter structures couple to the
gravity kinematics in a way that makes the deficit-matter and flat-rotation-curve results physical rather
than definitional.
