# AT-QG Natural Core Paper

**Status:** MINIMAL AT CORE — NATURAL OBSERVABLES ONLY

AT’s strongest minimal case is a small natural core fixed before observation and evaluated with three calibration anchors: `m_e`, `v`, and SI conversion conventions. The natural observables are the family count, `θ_QCD = 0`, the blind Higgs reconstruction, the Higgs mass `M_H`, and the pre-registered targets `P1`, `P2`, and `P3`. These are the observables for which the theory is least exposed to catalog-driven target selection and most directly tied to the internal D96 structure.

The resulting case is deliberately narrow. It excludes secondary catalog matches and post-hoc observables, and it does not claim global uniqueness for the full 29-observable register. The claim is instead that AT has a compact pre-registered nucleus with explicit anchors and no fit parameters, and that this nucleus survives as the most defensible minimal version of the theory.
