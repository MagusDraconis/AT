## 4. Why Non-Zero Information Appears

The uniform state is only the **expected** profile of the actualization. The
actualization process is a **discrete counting** process: its mandatory Poisson
fluctuations (QG15/30) mean the realized counts differ from the mean. This:

1. **breaks the permutation symmetry** of the uniform state (one specific
   branching history is realized);
2. **differentiates the branches** (per-generation realized populations differ);
3. **creates an entropy deficit** — I = ln K − H = KL(ρ‖uniform) > 0;
4. **forms a record** — the octave-occupied spectral structure [4,4,87].

Information appears because actualization is not a smooth flow: it is a
discrete process whose intrinsic fluctuations generate the non-uniformity
that carries information. No information is imported — it is generated by the
actualization dynamics itself from the zero-information initial state.
