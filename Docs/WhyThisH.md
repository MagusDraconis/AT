# Why This H (QG-100)

Why does reality contain H ≈ 2.2×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹ instead of another value?

## The selection landscape
The cosmic age scales as t ∝ 1/H. Each process needs a minimum age: chemistry ~0.01 Gyr,
stars ~0.1 Gyr, galaxies ~0.5 Gyr, complex life ~3 Gyr. Varying H/H0 over 1e-6 .. 1e6:

- H ≳ 10× H0 (t ≲ 1.4 Gyr): complex life impossible.
- H ≳ 100× H0 (t ≲ 140 Myr): stars marginal.
- H ≳ 1000× H0 (t ≲ 14 Myr): no stars.

The anthropic window (for complex life) is log(H/H0) ∈ [−6, +0.66], a span of ~2.7 decades.

## Random vs selected
The age constraint is only an UPPER bound on H (H ≲ ~5×H0 for complex life, ≲ ~100×H0 for
stars); there is NO lower bound from age (a smaller H gives an older universe). So the life
window spans ~6–7 decades, and a log-uniform H lands in it ~50% of the time. The observed H is
NOT fine-tuned — it is essentially arbitrary within a huge range, constrained only from above.

## Λ ~ H²
The causal-set Λ ~ H²/c² makes the 'why now' coincidence automatic: Λ and H track each other,
so there is ONE selection (H), not two. This removes the dark-energy fine-tuning but does not
explain H.

## Verdict
Levels 1–4 pass; Level 5 fails. H ≈ 2.2e-18 s⁻¹ is NOT special — it is arbitrary within a huge
window, weakly (if at all) selected. H is the single unexplained input (consistent with QG-099:
H is primitive).
