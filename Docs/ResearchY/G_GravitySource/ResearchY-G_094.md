## Defects found while building this audit

1. **A saturating function's threshold read off a local slope.** `DecisiveCoefficient()` first computed the crossover
   as `separation ÷ (signature at 0.3 ÷ 0.3)`. The signature is **not linear** in the coefficient — it is
   `exp(−x)·expm1(−c x²/2)`, which **saturates** — so the local slope **over**estimates the sensitivity and the
   threshold came out **4.6 % too low** (**3.232** against **3.386** by bisection). It is now found by **bisection**,
   and the residual is reported (`< 1e-6` of the separation) so the root is verified rather than assumed.
   *This is a variant of rule 6 (compute the quantity you quote), not a new defect class, so no new guard and no new
   rule was added — the judgement is recorded here rather than left implicit.*
2. **Two columns that would have told the wrong story.** The neutron-star row first reported its "change" as the
   **gap to the separation** (**1.172E-001**) while flagging `moves = false`, which reads as a large change that does
   not count; it now reports the **signature itself** (0 → 8.405E-003) and puts the decisiveness in the note. The
   cosmology row reported its change as a bare number; it now states the **ratio** to the redshift's change, which is
   what makes "no cosmological observable registers it" a measurement.
3. **A boolean threshold that could have hidden a real change.** The cosmology flag used a `1e-9` cut, so a change of
   **1.666E-010** was `false` — correct, but a reader would not know *how far* below. The note now carries the ratio
   (**eight orders of magnitude**), so the flag is a summary rather than the evidence.
4. **A pattern-match assertion on a field that could name another row.** The first draft asserted the curl form's
   longitudinal count was **1**; the measurement is **0** (the curl form **removes** the longitudinal mode). The
   assertion was inverted because the reading was assumed instead of read.

## What this does and does not establish

**Establishes:** that the closure's effect is **concentrated in one gap** and is **zero** in the other three; that the
concentration is measured on the readouts (**7 of 8** move under the memory channel and **0** under every other form);
that the **same** gap changes what the theory can **do**, so the observable gain and the manipulation gain have one
carrier; that the neutron-star verdict becomes decisive only above a **measured** coefficient; and that the closure is
**invisible at the mean density**, so a **cosmology consequence never appears**.

**Does not establish** — and this is the audit's central caveat — **that the closures deliver anything.** The forms are
**hypothetical**, so every figure is **conditional on the value the closure delivers**. For the memory channel the
condition is explicit and measured: the theory currently carries coefficient **zero** (the rate is `exp(A)`, whose
signature is **exactly 0.0**), so a closure returning the **same** value changes **nothing** and only the derivation
improves. The figures above are at the **window's edge** — the smallest coefficient the data could still hide. Nor does
the audit claim the three mathematical gaps are unimportant: the vector sector is a **physics gap for the group that
owns it**, and the audit says so rather than calling it elegance.

**Why this is not a restatement of G_093:** G_093 measured which structures are **required and absent**. This audit asks
what happens if they are **present**, which requires **constructing** the forms, **recomputing six readout sets**, and
**separating** the one gap that moves a readout from the three that do not — a distinction G_093's closure column
cannot make, because all three are equally closure-necessary there.
