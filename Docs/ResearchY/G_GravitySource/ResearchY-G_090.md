## Question

**Assumed true:** direct control of the density `ρ` exists — a process that changes `ρ` **without moving ordinary mass**.
The audit determines **what mechanism it would require**, inside the surviving sector's vocabulary. **Ordinary mass
relocation is excluded as an *answer*** (it is what must be surpassed), adding primitives at the outset is forbidden, and
arguing from previous negative audits is forbidden.

**Success:** a physically real process that changes `ρ` without ordinary mass redistribution.
**Failure:** every candidate collapses to mass transport or to an unactuatable variable.

## 1. The inverse requirement (task 1) — derived

| components | change space | image rank | nullity | conserving | complement | all conserving changes are transports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 96 | **95** | 1 | **95** | **0** | **yes** |
| 2 | 96 | 94 | 1 | 95 | **1** | no |
| 4 | 96 | 92 | 1 | 95 | **3** | no |

**On a connected substrate the transports span every conserving change**, so a conserving change of the density **is
always a redistribution** — ordinary mass motion — and the only change left **outside** is a **non-divergence
operation**. The complement is `0` on the connected substrate and one per cut on a divided one. **The requirement is
therefore exact: the actuator must produce a change outside the image of the class's divergence, or act on an assumed
input or a parameter of the law.**

The clock's response to the density is **compressive**: a fractional density change produces **1/d** of the clock change
— so the density must move *more* than the clock, which is the price of the channel.

## 3. The conservation audit (task 3)

On a **connected** substrate redistribution and local creation **coincide in the small**: there is no third option. On a
**cut** substrate a complement of **`c − 1`** appears — conserving changes that are **not** transports, each changing a
component's total while conserving the global one — and those are exactly the changes the ledger flags. So **effective
local creation without mass transport exists in the structure and requires a cut substrate** (an assumed input, G_080)
plus an operation the class does not contain.

## Defects found while building this audit

1. **The oscillation-frequency row contradicted itself.** It was labelled *arity-excluded* while its own flags
   classified it as a **shape-function** (a frequency *is* a property of the occupancy's motion), so the candidate split
   and the row's measurement string disagreed. The **row** was fixed rather than the assertion — and the corrected
   measurement says the honest thing: a frequency is a property of the occupancy, and the one frequency-like lever the
   sector has (the uniform direction) is what count conservation forbids.
2. **The candidate-split assertions carried literal counts** (`7 / 2 / 1`) that the measurement corrected to
   **`8 / 1 / 1`**. The rule this project enforces is that a literal must not reach a verdict — and it applies to the
   *test's* expectations as much as to the audit's.
3. **A cross-check referenced another audit's type** (G_087's observable record where G_088's catalogue was meant),
   caught by the compiler. Recorded because it is the third scripted-edit slip in this chain: the tooling is part of the
   provenance.

## What this does and does not establish

**Establishes.** That on a connected substrate the conserving changes and the transports are the **same set**, so a
conserving density change cannot avoid being a redistribution; that the ten listed mechanisms split into **functions of
the distribution** (which redistribute) and **quantities the clock cannot take** (which cannot reach it); that an
oscillation's net effect is **zero by averaging**; that the sector has **no energy functional** for the density; and that
the map `ρ → clock` is **compressive**.

**Does not establish.** That a direct density control is **impossible** — the audit was asked to assume it and to find
the mechanism, and it does: **the requirement is a non-divergence operation, or an actuator for an assumed input**.

**Why the collapse is a result rather than a refusal:** the ten candidates fail for **two different and measured
reasons** — being the density's own shape, or being invisible to a clock whose signature takes the density alone — and
the split is reported **per candidate** rather than as a verdict in bulk.

**Out of scope (substrate).** No spectral quantity is quoted — so the D96/D96³ rule is **not engaged**.
