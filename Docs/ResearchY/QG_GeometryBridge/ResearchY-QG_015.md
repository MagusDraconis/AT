## Purpose

**Why does an observable world exist?** Is observability a NECESSARY consequence of
Difference, or an independent boundary condition? The chain Difference →
Distinguishability produces states; Actualization evolves them; measurement reads
them (M_001). This audit asks whether the READ is forced by the primitives — whether
a distinguishable universe MUST become observable — or whether observability is an
additional requirement. The answer determines whether the observable world is
DERIVED, EMERGENT, or BOUNDARY.

## 4. Does Difference alone produce observation?

**NO.** Difference → distinguishability (D_039) produces the STATES. Observation
requires an ACTUALIZATION EVENT that READS them (M_001) — a separate act. The
distinguishable state space exists without any read. Difference produces the
possibility of observation, not the observation itself.

**U1 is the demonstration:** the 95 states, ρ, g, I_occ, and ΩΛ all exist with no
event reading them.

---

## 5. Does Actualization necessarily produce observable outcomes?

**PARTIALLY.** Actualization advances the phase per tick (Δθ = 2πk/N, D_041) — it
produces EVOLUTION. An "observable outcome" is a READ of the state (which state is
realized). Actualization alone produces the trajectory; observability is the
READOUT capability (M_001). Actualization necessarily produces ticks; it does NOT
necessarily produce a read (U1: evolution without observation).

## 7. Search: minimal principle generating observable reality

The minimal principle is the OBSERVABLE-SECTOR requirement (D_020): the Z2-paired
complex sector — the requirement that every frequency carry both quadratures
([magnitude, phase], D_036/D_037) so that states are READABLE. This is the input
that makes the world observable:

- states must be complex (both quadratures readable) — the Z2-paired sector (D_020)
- every eigenvalue must have multiplicity ≥ 2 — complete pairing (D_035, derived
  from complex observability)
- a measurement event reads both quadratures of one mode (M_001)

**Observable reality requires the observable-sector input — it is not produced by
Difference alone.**

## 9. Prove or refute: a distinguishable universe must become observable

**REFUTED.** A distinguishable universe does NOT necessarily become observable:

1. Difference → distinguishability produces the states (D_039).
2. Geometry (g = ρ^(2/d)η), information (I_occ = 0.7513), and cosmology
   (ΩΛ = 0.6839) are all DERIVED from the state space and exist WITHOUT observation
   (U1).
3. Observation (M_001) is an actualization event that READS a state — a separate
   act, not a consequence of Difference.
4. U1 (distinguishable, unobserved) is fully consistent: all physics exists, no
   read occurs.
5. The OBSERVABLE world requires the observable-sector input (D_020: Z2-paired
   complex sector) — an independent boundary condition.

Therefore: a distinguishable universe CAN remain unobserved (U1), and becoming
observable requires the observable-sector boundary input. Observability is EMERGENT
(capability) under a BOUNDARY (the complex-readability requirement) — not DERIVED.

## 10. Necessity Proof

Observability is NOT a necessary consequence of Difference: a distinguishable
universe (U1) is fully consistent — geometry, information, and cosmology exist with
no observation. Observation is NECESSARY only for the READ (which state is actual)

is necessary FOR the experienced world but not FOR physics in general — it is
EMERGENT (capability) under a BOUNDARY (requirement).
