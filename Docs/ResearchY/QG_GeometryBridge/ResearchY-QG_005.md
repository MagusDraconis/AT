## Purpose

**Why does count structure generate geometry?** QG_004 established that ρ is
fundamentally a count structure, with geometry and information as its derived faces.
This audit asks WHY: is geometry a necessary consequence of distinguishability
counting, or an independent postulate?

## 5. The minimal principle generating g = ρ^(2/d)η

**Measure preservation (QG207).** The metric g = ρ^(2/d)η is the UNIQUE conformal-flat
metric that preserves the counting measure:

```
√(−g) = ρ^(kd/2) = ρ  ⟹  k = 2/d
```

The derived geodesic acceleration gives the same: a = −(1/d)ρ′/ρ ⟹ k/2 = 1/d ⟹
k = 2/d (QG207). **The unique exponent is forced by requiring the metric to preserve
the count.**

## 6. Why a density becomes a ruler

**Because the ruler must preserve the count.** The metric is the geometric object that
keeps the counting measure ρ as its volume element (√(−g) = ρ). A density becomes a
ruler because the ruler is REQUIRED to measure the density's own volume. Geometry is
not an arbitrary postulate — it is the unique conformal structure that conserves the
count.
