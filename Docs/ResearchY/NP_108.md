## Purpose

The NP arc (NP_055–NP_107) built the full ontology — Difference → actualization → D96 → matter →
particles → forces → cosmology → structure → existence. NP_108 asks the closing epistemic
question: **what observation would directly falsify Actualization Theory?** Program: (1) inventory
every core claim; (2) identify the observation/experiment/measurement for each; (3) determine what
would have to be observed for the theory to fail; (4) locate the strongest prediction, weakest
prediction, and strongest vulnerability. **Success criterion:** a ranked list — "if X is observed,
AT is wrong." No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

## 2. Strongest prediction — where AT is most exposed

**The strongest prediction is the tightest numerical one: n_s = 0.96497 (0.007%)**, with ℓ₁ = 220.48
(0.008%) and ΩΛ = 0.6839 (0.12%) close behind. These are DERIVED, single-valued, and pinned to
existing high-precision observations — any future measurement outside their windows falsifies AT.

**The strongest *uniquely-AT* prediction is AT-P042 (the discrete tick phase lattice)** — the only
prediction absent from standard QM (M_009). It is mathematically exact (the lattice N/gcd(N,k)) but
experimentally in-principle only (the tick scale is uncalibrated).

---

## 3. Weakest prediction — where AT is most robust

The **weakest (least falsifiable) predictions are the ontological claims** — that particles are
resonance classes, forces are generator actions, matter is the deficit, and Difference is the
primitive. These are structural (not single-valued): they are falsified only by an object *not
expressible* in the ontology, which is a weaker, less sharp test than a numerical window.
