## Observations

1. **Localized disturbance (O1):** Difference is the counting difference from a uniform
   background; a compact deviation of ρ (or a Q-event) is a legitimate *configuration* of
   Difference. The zero mode is the uniform rest state; the positive modes are the
   deviations. Difference is not *defined as* a disturbance — the disturbance is a
   configuration of the primitive.
2. **Generation propagation (O2):** Actualization spreads count through the generation
   tree: ρ_k = μ^k/S, S = Σ_{j<K} μ^j, with μ^k the root-to-generation-k path multiplicity.
   This is propagation in generation space. Spatial propagation is null-geodesic, n = 1,
   conformally invariant — no refractive medium exists (QG21, QG28, QG212).
3. **Standing-wave identity (O3):** The eigenmodes of the circulant graph Laplacian are the
   discrete Fourier modes φ_k(n) = e^{2πikn/96}: the 95 positive modes are the standing
   normal modes of the closed ring; ω_k = √λ_k are the mode frequencies.
4. **Ring degeneracy (O4):** λ_k = λ_{96−k}; the Z2 doublet pairing (95/95, QG153) is the
   ±k degeneracy of a ring — a natural wave-mechanical signature supporting the
   weak-isospin doublet structure as a ring-mode degeneracy (supporting interpretation).
5. **No spatial center (O5):** the attractor is a translation-invariant circulant ring;
   there is no distinguished site. A source exists only as the branching root (generation
   0); ladder radii (6.0–17.333) and r₀ = ln(span) are derived center-relative quantities.
6. **Spectral 2π from closure (O6):** λ_k = 2Σ(1−cos 2πdk/96) uses 2π through the roots of
   unity e^{2πik/96}; for a closed ring of N sites the circle constant is the periodicity
   constant of the closure. This is an observation about the canonical spectral layer; it
   does not resolve the Bekenstein boundary (QG196 unchanged).
7. **Wave vocabulary already present (O7):** radius (ladder radii), circumference (N = 96
   ring sites), wavelength (mode index k ↔ 96/k), frequency (ω_k = √λ_k), resonance
   (degeneracy groups, spectral gaps, octave bands) are all existing derived/structural
   quantities.
8. **Circular topology and phase (O8):** the attractor is a closed ring and the state-phase
   lattice θ_k = 2πk/96 is a discrete circle; circular *structure* and *phase order* are
   emergent. Circular *propagation dynamics* (a traveling disturbance around the ring) is
