## Purpose

Attempt to **falsify** the ResearchY-A_003 conclusion

> **Actualization is branching plus spectral projection.**

by testing whether any alternative propagation model generates the D96 resonance
structure independently. The audit is referee-hostile: it tries to break the conclusion
under the strictest constraints — accepted D96 structure only, no fitting, no target
occupancies, no new assumptions.

## Conclusion under attack (A_003)

- **Branching** (ρ_k = μ^k/S) carries the count content (the magnitudes).
- **Spectral projection** (onto the attractor eigenbasis) carries the mode structure
  (ω_k, moments, span, octave bands [4,4,87]).
- The D96 resonance structure is *carried by the graph medium* and *read through the
  propagating count*, not generated by propagation.

---

## Falsification protocol

For each alternative model, test three targets WITHOUT fitting, WITHOUT target
occupancies, WITHOUT new assumptions:

1. **Occupancies:** does the model reproduce [4,4,87] from its own content?
2. **λ structure:** does the model reproduce the Laplacian spectrum λ_k
   (= 2Σ(1−cos 2πdk/96)) from its own content?
3. **Moments:** does the model reproduce the spectrum moments (Σ√m = 64.08,
   Σm = 95, Σm² = 229, occMom = 1900.25)?

An alternative *falsifies* the conclusion only if it passes all three from its own
content. Passing by reading the spectrum is not falsification — it is the spectral-
projection half of the conclusion wearing a different costume.

## Alternatives tested

### Alternative A — Branching generates occupancies

- Model: ρ_k = μ^k/S (scalar, 1-D generation shares).
- Occupancies: the shares are a geometric sequence over generations — they have **no
  octave-band structure**. At μ = 1 (canonical criticality) all shares are equal
  (1/K each); at μ = 2 the scaled shares are ≈ [0.4, 0.7, 1.5, 3.0, 6.0, 11.9, 23.8,
  47.7] — neither is [4,4,87]. To reach three octave bands one must add a banding rule
  (a new assumption) and/or tune μ (fitting). **FAILS.**
- λ structure: branching is one-dimensional; it carries no mode index, hence no λ_k.
  **FAILS.**
- Moments: no multiset [42×2,5,6]; the shares are not the multiplicity moments.
  **FAILS.**

### Alternative B — Diffusion generates occupancies

- Model: ρ(t) = e^{−tL}ρ(0) = Σ_k e^{−tλ_k} c_k φ_k (heat kernel on C96).
- Occupancies: the diffusion output is a smooth profile over sites; it relaxes to the
  uniform zero mode (erases structure, A_003). A threshold count of "dominant" modes
  (e^{−tλ} > ½) gives 3 modes at t = 1 — not [4,4,87] — and t is a free parameter.
  The octave bands require counting ω_k — a spectral read. **FAILS.**
- λ structure: the operator is a function of L; it presupposes λ_k, does not generate
  them. **FAILS.**
- Moments: presupposed (the heat trace is a function of the spectrum). **FAILS.**

### Alternative C — Wave propagation generates occupancies

- Model: modes oscillate at ω_k = √λ_k (the wave frequencies ARE the spectral
  frequencies).
- Occupancies: counting ω_k in octave bands gives [4,4,87] **exactly** — but only
  because the wave operator is built from √L, i.e., the octave count IS the spectral
  projection. The wave model adds non-canonical dynamics (a derived wave equation does
  not exist, A_001 OP1) and still reproduces the bands only by reading the spectrum.
  **FAILS as independent generation** (reproduces by spectral reading = confirms the
  spectral half, not an alternative to it).
- λ structure: presupposed (ω_k = √λ_k). **FAILS.**
- Moments: presupposed. **FAILS.**

### Alternative D — Hybrid propagation generates occupancies

- Model: any coupling of branching shares with spectral/diffusion/wave content.
- Occupancies: a hybrid that combines generation shares with octave content requires a
  coupling constant (branching × spectral weight) — a free parameter to be fit, or a
  new assumption. With no fitting and no new assumptions, no hybrid reproduces [4,4,87].
  **FAILS.**
- λ structure: the spectral factor presupposes λ_k. **FAILS.**
- Moments: presupposed. **FAILS.**

## Research Conclusions

**Falsification verdict: FAILED — the conclusion survives.**

No alternative — branching (A), diffusion (B), wave (C), or hybrid (D) — reproduces the
D96 resonance structure (occupancies + λ structure + moments) from its own content
under the stated constraints:

1. **λ structure** is reproduced by NO model: every propagation operator (L, e^{−tL},
   √L) is a function of the graph Laplacian. The spectrum is the *input medium*, not an
   output. This forces the spectral half of the conclusion.
2. **[4,4,87]** is reproduced by the wave model only by reading ω_k octaves — which IS
   the spectral projection. It is not generated by wave dynamics. This confirms, rather
   than falsifies, the spectral half.
3. **Count content** is carried canonically only by branching (ρ_k = μ^k/S): diffusion
   and wave are non-canonical dynamics, and their spectra presuppose the graph.
4. **Moments** are the multiplicity-multiset moments of the spectrum — no propagation
   model generates the multiset [42×2,5,6].

**Uniqueness verdict: the decomposition "branching + spectral projection" is UNIQUE
within the accepted D96 structure — not merely preferred.** The count content has
exactly one canonical generator (branching); the mode/occupancy content has exactly one
source (the graph spectrum). The two are forced by the structure, and every alternative
either presupposes the spectrum, adds a free parameter, or requires new dynamics.

**Scoping (referee-safe).** "Unique" means: within the accepted D96 structure, with no
fitting, no target occupancies, and no new assumptions, no alternative decomposition
reproduces all three targets. The wave model reproduces the occupancies trivially by
reading the spectrum; this does not distinguish the models — which is why the full
three-target test is the discriminator.

**Success criterion verdict.** The falsification attempt does NOT overturn
ResearchY-A_003. **Actualization = branching plus spectral projection** is the unique
canonical decomposition: branching for the count magnitudes, spectral projection for the
mode structure, with the graph as the medium. No canonical value was changed.

4. **Wave-operator minimality.** If a derived wave operator on the attractor ever
   existed, would its octave content differ from the plain spectral count? (Currently
   the wave frequencies are exactly ω_k, so it would not.)
