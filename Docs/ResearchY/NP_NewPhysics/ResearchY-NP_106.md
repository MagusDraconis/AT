## Purpose

NP_104/105 established that Difference exists, is conserved, and cannot vanish — yet reality is
dynamic (there are events, ticks). NP_106 asks the question that tension makes unavoidable: **why
must Difference actualize?** Program: (1) remove actualization; (2) test a universe with Difference
but no actualization; (3) determine whether counting / information / structure / existence survive;
(4) determine whether actualization is derived / independent / logical necessity / emergent; (5)
find the minimal reason a conserved Difference produces events. **Success criterion:** determine why
a world of pure Difference does not remain static. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

---

## 1. Remove actualization

```
remove actualization (no tick, no events):
   → the distinctions are never DRAWN (never enacted)
   → no count realization (nothing to count)
   → no information (no distinguishability is enacted)
   → no structure (no realized patterns)
   → no existence (no persistence, NP_102)
```

**A universe of Difference with no actualization is incoherent.** Removing actualization does not
leave a "static Difference" — it leaves nothing at all, because a distinction that is never drawn
is not a distinction.

---

## 2. The contradiction of a "static Difference"

**"Static Difference" is a contradiction in terms.** A Difference is a relation of contrast —
"this, not that" — and a contrast is *defined by* its being drawn. An un-drawn distinction is not
a distinction; it collapses into undifferentiated unity — which is the absence of Difference
(refuted by NP_104: Difference cannot vanish). Therefore:

```
Difference  =  an ACT of distinguishing, not a static state
```

Difference's binary content ("this ≠ that") is *inherently* enacted: to differ IS to draw the
distinction. A "pure static Difference" is impossible — it would be a distinction that never
distinguishes.

## 3. Do counting / information / structure / existence survive?

| Quantity | Survives without actualization? |
|---|---|
| counting | **NO** — nothing is drawn, so there is nothing to count |
| information | **NO** — distinguishability is enacted, not latent |
| structure | **NO** — no realized patterns |
| existence | **NO** — existence = Difference + Persistence (NP_102); no persistence without the tick |

**Nothing survives.** Actualization is not an *addition* to Difference — it is what Difference
*is* (its dynamic face).

## 4. A / B / C / D — what is actualization?

| Determination | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A) derived from Difference** | **PARTIAL — the act.** The ACT of distinguishing follows from Difference (a Difference must be drawn); but the discrete FORM (the tick) does not. |
| **B) independent primitive** | **REFUTED.** Actualization is not a separate primitive — it is the dynamic face of Difference. |
| **C) logical necessity** | **YES — the answer.** A static Difference is contradictory, so Difference *necessarily* enacts the distinction; that enactment IS actualization. |
| **D) emergent consequence** | **PARTIAL — the dynamics.** The sequence of events (the dynamics) is emergent; the act of actualization is not (it is built into Difference). |

**Determination: C (logical necessity) — with A (the act is derived) and D (the dynamics is
emergent) as partial readings; B (independent primitive) is refuted.**

## 5. The minimal reason

> **A Difference is an act, not a state.** To differ is to *enact* the distinction. A conserved
> Difference that never acted would not be a Difference — it would be an undifferentiated unity.
> Therefore the very nature of Difference (its binary contrast) *is* its actualization, and the
> tick is the discrete pulse of that enactment.

```
Difference (content: "this ≠ that")        [the WHAT — BOUNDARY, NP_086]
   → actualization (drawing the distinction)  [the ACT — logical necessity, C]
      → tick (one outcome per tick)            [the discrete FORM — BOUNDARY residue]
```

The tick is not a separate primitive: it is the discreteness of Difference's self-enactment. The
minimal reason a conserved Difference produces events is that **a distinction must be drawn to be
a distinction**.
