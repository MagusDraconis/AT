## Purpose

NP_117 established purpose = the selected prediction (the aim). NP_118 asks the final agential
question: **what is choice, and how does it differ from purpose and action?** Program: (1) define
alternative / option / choice / decision / action; (2) determine whether choice is selection among
predictions / value maximization / constrained actualization / purpose becoming action; (3) remove
alternatives; (4) remove values; (5) remove purpose; (6) determine what survives; (7) trace meaning
→ purpose → choice → action; (8) find the minimum condition for a genuine choice. **Success
criterion:** the ontology of choice, distinguished from purpose and action. No new primitives;
canonical AT unchanged.

---

## 1. Define the five terms

| Term | In AT |
|---|---|
| **alternative** | a possible future actualization (an option) |
| **option** | a weighted possibility (a distinction weighted by value, NP_116) |
| **choice** | the ACT of selecting — the transition from purpose to action (value-guided) |
| **decision** | the settled selection (the outcome of choosing) |
| **action** | the execution — the future actualization (the result) |

**Choice is the ACT; purpose is the AIM; action is the RESULT.**

---

## 2. A / B / C / D — what is choice?

| Interpretation | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A) selection among predictions** | **YES — the act.** Choice IS the selection among weighted predictions. |
| **B) value maximization** | **PARTIAL.** Choice selects value-guided, but it does not necessarily *maximize* — it picks among the weighted alternatives. |
| **C) constrained actualization** | **YES — the same object.** Choice is actualization constrained by the value hierarchy and the available alternatives. |
| **D) purpose becoming action** | **YES — the transition.** Choice is the moment purpose (the aim) becomes action (the doing). |

**Determination: A = C = D (selection among predictions = constrained actualization = purpose
becoming action); B (value maximization) is partial.**

---

## 3. Remove alternatives

**Choice collapses.** With no alternatives there is nothing to select among — the actualization is
forced, not chosen. (Purpose survives — the aim remains; but there is no choice to reach it.)

---

## 4. Remove values

**Choice collapses; purpose and meaning collapse.** Without values there is no selection criterion —
the selection is random (a raw Born draw, NP_093), not a choice. Knowledge and understanding survive.

---

## 5. Remove purpose

**Choice collapses.** Without an aim there is no direction to select toward — the selection is just
prediction, not choice. Meaning survives (it weighs), but it does not choose.

---

## 6. What survives?

| Removed | Survives | Gone |
|---|---|---|
| alternatives | purpose, meaning | **choice** (forced, not chosen) |
| values | knowledge, understanding | **choice, purpose, meaning** (random, not chosen) |
| purpose | meaning | **choice** (prediction, not choice) |

**Choice is the most dependent** — it needs alternatives (to select among), values (to select by),
and purpose (to select toward).

---

## 7. Meaning → purpose → choice → action

```
meaning (weighs the significance, NP_116)
   → purpose (selects the aim, NP_117)       [the direction]
      → choice (the act of selecting)          [the transition]
         → action (the future actualization)    [the result]
```

Choice is the **bridge**: it is where the aim (purpose) becomes the doing (action), by selecting one
alternative under the value weighting.

---

## 8. The minimum condition for a genuine choice

**ALTERNATIVES + VALUES + PURPOSE.** A genuine choice requires all three: something to choose among
(the alternatives), a criterion to choose by (the values), and an aim to choose toward (the
purpose). This is what separates choice from the raw Born selection (NP_093), which has alternatives
and weights but no aim: actualization is a *draw*, choice is a *selection toward an aim*.

## 9. Counterexamples

| Attempt | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "choice = value maximization" | choice selects, it does not necessarily maximize (it picks among the weighted alternatives) |
| "choice = raw actualization" | actualization is a draw (no aim); choice is a selection toward an aim |
| "choice = purpose" | purpose is the aim (direction); choice is the act (selection) |
| "choice = action" | action is the result (execution); choice is the act (selection) |
| "choice survives without alternatives" | nothing to select among → forced, not chosen |

---

## 10. Falsification paths

| Claim | Falsification |
|---|---|
| choice = the act of selecting | a choice that selects nothing (no alternative picked) |
| the minimum condition is alternatives + values + purpose | a genuine choice missing one of the three |
| choice ≠ raw actualization | a choice identical to the raw Born draw (no aim) |

**Conclusion.** Choice is the **act of selecting** — purpose becoming action: the observer selects
one alternative, value-guided, toward its aim. It is distinct from purpose (the direction) and action

draw, choice is a selection toward an aim. The minimum condition is alternatives + values + purpose.
This completes the agential chain meaning → purpose → choice → action. No new primitive; canonical AT
unchanged.
