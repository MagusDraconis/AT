## Purpose

NP_127 established coherence engineering. NP_128 asks the resource question: **is coherence a
physical resource?** Program: (1) inventory phenomena where coherence increases capability /
decoherence decreases it; (2) compare information / energy / entropy / coherence; (3) determine
whether coherence is a measurable resource / bookkeeping quantity / hidden information / independent
capability; (4) search for fundamental limits (lifetime / density / transfer); (5) identify
technologies benefiting from maximizing coherence; (6) test whether a conservation law or trade-off
exists (coherence ↔ entropy). **Success criterion:** determine whether coherence is the most
practically valuable quantity revealed by the ontology. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

## 6. The trade-off: coherence ↔ entropy

```
coherence  (locked phase, low entropy)  ⇌  entropy  (decohered, high entropy)
   friction (NP_095) converts coherence into entropy (NP_096)  — the coherence budget
```

**There is a fundamental trade-off, not a conservation law**: coherence is ORDER (low entropy), and
entropy is DISORDER (decoherence). Friction (NP_095) spends coherence to produce entropy (NP_096) —
exactly as work degrades to heat. Coherence is therefore a **resource with a budget**: you spend it
to do phase-locked work, and it decoheres. It is not conserved — it is *degradable*.
