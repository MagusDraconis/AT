## Purpose

NP_124 completed the individual arc with wisdom. NP_125 asks the social question: **what is
cooperation, and how does it differ from individual purpose?** Program: (1) define self / other /
cooperation / conflict; (2) determine whether cooperation is aligned meanings / shared purposes /
integrated self-models / multi-observer optimization; (3) compare isolated / competing / cooperating
observers; (4) test responsibility, freedom, and meaning in multi-observer systems. **Success
criterion:** the ontology of cooperation, distinguished from individual purpose. No new primitives;
canonical AT unchanged.

---

## 1. Define the four terms

| Term | In AT |
|---|---|
| **self** | one persistent Difference structure (an observer, NP_111) |
| **other** | another persistent Difference structure |
| **cooperation** | MULTIPLE observers' aligned meanings and shared purposes, mutually modeled |
| **conflict** | MULTIPLE observers' misaligned meanings and conflicting purposes |

**Cooperation and conflict are two multi-observer relations** — alignment or misalignment of
meanings and purposes.

---

## 2. A / B / C / D — what is cooperation?

| Interpretation | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A) aligned meanings** | **YES — the values.** Cooperation requires shared value-weightings (NP_116). |
| **B) shared purposes** | **YES — the aims.** Cooperation requires aligned selected predictions (NP_117). |
| **C) integrated self-models** | **YES — the mutual modeling.** Each observer models the OTHER's meaning and purpose. |
| **D) multi-observer optimization** | **PARTIAL — a consequence.** Cooperation is mutual modeling + alignment, not mere optimization. |

**Determination: A = B = C (aligned meanings = shared purposes = integrated self-models — the three
necessary conditions); D (multi-observer optimization) is a partial consequence.**

---

## 3. Isolated / competing / cooperating observers

| Configuration | Relation of purposes |
|---|---|
| **isolated observer** | ONE purpose (individual, NP_117) |
| **competing observers** | CONFLICTING purposes (meanings misaligned) → conflict |
| **cooperating observers** | ALIGNED purposes (meanings shared) → cooperation |

**Individual purpose is one observer's selected prediction; cooperation is multiple observers'
aligned selected predictions.**

---

## 4. Responsibility / freedom / meaning in multi-observer systems

| Concept | In a cooperating system |
|---|---|
| **meaning** | ALIGNED — the observers share value-weightings (NP_116) |
| **freedom** | RETAINED — each observer chooses voluntarily; cooperation is aligned choice, not force |
| **responsibility** | DISTRIBUTED — each observer owns its part of the shared purpose (NP_120) |

**Cooperation does not eliminate freedom or responsibility** — it aligns them. Each observer remains
free and responsible; cooperation is the *alignment* of their choices.

---

## 5. The social extension of the self-model

```
consciousness  = the model of SELF (NP_122)     [the loop inward]
cooperation    = the model of OTHER + alignment  [the loop outward]
```

Cooperation is the social extension of the self-model: where consciousness is the observer modeling
itself, cooperation is observers modeling *each other* and aligning. The same Difference ontology
scales from one observer to many.
