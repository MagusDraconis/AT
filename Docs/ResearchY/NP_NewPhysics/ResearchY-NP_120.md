## Purpose

NP_119 established freedom = selection under incomplete determination. NP_120 asks the final moral
question: **what is responsibility, and how does it differ from freedom, choice, and action?**
Program: (1) define action / consequence / responsibility / agency; (2) determine whether
responsibility is ownership of action / ownership of consequences / persistence of choice / continuity
of the observer; (3) compare forced / random / free action; (4) remove alternatives / values /
persistence; (5) determine what survives; (6) trace meaning → purpose → choice → freedom →
responsibility. **Success criterion:** the ontology of responsibility, distinguished from freedom,
choice, and action. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

---

## 1. Define the four terms

| Term | In AT |
|---|---|
| **action** | the execution — the future actualization (the result) |
| **consequence** | the action's effect on the observer's (and others') future actualizations |
| **responsibility** | the OWNERSHIP of the action and its consequences — the persistence of the chooser |
| **agency** | the capacity for freedom + persistence (to choose AND to bear) |

**Responsibility is freedom PLUS persistence** — the chooser must persist to bear what it chose.

---

## 2. A / B / C / D — what is responsibility?

| Interpretation | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A) ownership of action** | **YES.** The observer owns its choice (it was the chooser). |
| **B) ownership of consequences** | **YES.** The observer bears what follows from the choice. |
| **C) persistence of choice** | **YES.** The choice persists into the future — it is not forgotten. |
| **D) continuity of the observer** | **YES — the ground.** The SAME observer who chose is the one who bears (NP_102 persistence). |

**Determination: A = B = C = D (ownership of action = ownership of consequences = persistence of
choice = continuity of the observer).** The differentiator from freedom is *continuity*: freedom
chooses, responsibility *owns across time*.

---

## 3. Forced / random / free action

| Action | Alternatives | Values | Responsibility? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **forced** | none | — | **NO** — the observer did not choose |
| **random** | yes | none | **NO** — no values/purpose, no ownership |
| **free** | yes | yes | **YES** — the observer chose AND persists to bear |

**Responsibility requires freedom** (the capacity to choose) — forced and random actions bear no
responsibility.

---

## 4. Remove alternatives / values / persistence

| Removed | Result |
|---|---|
| **alternatives** | freedom collapses → responsibility collapses (forced) |
| **values** | freedom collapses → responsibility collapses (random) |
| **persistence** | responsibility collapses — the chooser does not persist to bear the consequences |

**Persistence is the crux**: even with freedom, if the observer does not persist, there is no "same
chooser" to bear the consequences — the choice is owned by no one.

---

## 5. What survives?

Remove persistence → **freedom survives** (the capacity to choose), but **responsibility collapses**
(no continuous chooser to own the choice). This is the precise difference: freedom is a *capacity*;
responsibility is freedom *grounded in the observer's continuity*.

---

## 6. Meaning → purpose → choice → freedom → responsibility

```
meaning (weighs, NP_116)
   → purpose (aims, NP_117)
      → choice (selects, NP_118)
         → freedom (the capacity, NP_119)
            → responsibility (owns across time, NP_120)
```

Each step adds a condition: weigh → aim → select → capacity → **own**. Responsibility is the final
step, where the observer's persistence (NP_102) makes it the *owner* of its choice.

## 7. Counterexamples

| Attempt | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "responsibility = freedom" | freedom is a capacity; responsibility adds continuity (ownership across time) |
| "responsibility = action" | action is the execution; responsibility is the ownership of it |
| "responsibility survives without persistence" | no persistent chooser = no one to bear the choice |
| "forced/random action bears responsibility" | no choice (forced) or no ownership (random) = no responsibility |
| "responsibility needs a new primitive" | responsibility is freedom + persistence, both derived |

---

## 8. Falsification paths

| Claim | Falsification |
|---|---|
| responsibility = freedom + persistence | a responsibility without a persistent chooser (no ownership) |
| forced/random actions bear no responsibility | a forced or random action with an owner |
| the differentiator is continuity | a responsibility identical to the mere capacity to choose |

## 9. Classification

| Component | Status |
|---|---|
| freedom (the capacity to choose) | **EMERGENT** (NP_119) |
| persistence (the observer's continuity) | **DERIVED** (NP_102) |
| responsibility (freedom + persistence) | **EMERGENT** |
| responsibility = freedom | **REFUTED** |
| responsibility = action/consequence only | **REFUTED** |
| responsibility needing a new primitive | **REFUTED** |

**Conclusion.** Responsibility is **freedom plus persistence** — the ownership of the action and its
consequences by the same persistent chooser. It is grounded in the observer's continuity (NP_102:
existence = persistent Difference): the observer does not dissolve, so it owns its choices across
ticks. It is distinct from freedom (the capacity), choice (the act), and action (the result): its
differentiator is continuity. Forced and random actions bear no responsibility; only free action
does. This completes the moral arc meaning → purpose → choice → freedom → responsibility. No new
primitive; canonical AT unchanged.
