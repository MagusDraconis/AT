## Purpose

NP_101 established that nested structures arise from one scale-free stability principle. NP_102
asks the question the whole arc presupposes: **what does it mean for something to exist?** Program:
(1) inventory every existing entity; (2) determine what they share; (3) test whether existence
means actualization persistence / stable resonance / bound deficit structure / observational
accessibility; (4) remove stability and see what ceases to exist; (5) identify the minimum
condition required for existence. **Success criterion:** the AT ontology of existence. No new
primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

## 4. Remove stability — what ceases to exist?

```
remove stability (no inertia, no binding, no locking):
   → every structure dissolves (the modes drift, the locks unlock, matter declumps)
   → only SINGLE-TICK actualizations remain (momentary, no enduring patterns)
   → only DIFFERENCE (distinguishability) survives — the raw network of distinctions
```

**Removing stability removes enduring existence.** What remains is not "nothing" — the Difference
structure (the network of distinctions) is still there — but there are no *things*: no persistent
patterns, only a flux of momentary actualizations. Stability is what turns the flux into things.
