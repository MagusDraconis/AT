## Purpose

NP_155/156 established that force chains can be reconfigured and steered, but only within the known
jammed-granular state space. NP_157 established that "organization" is AT's word for the known
defect/contact/microstructure state space — an INTERPRETATION, not a new ontology. NP_167 asks the
frontier question those results make unavoidable: **do there exist large-scale organization states
of matter/network structures that humanity has never intentionally attempted to create?** Program:
(1) inventory the known organization states; (2) ignore chemistry and composition — compare only
organization; (3) search for theoretically allowed states with high coherence, high phase alignment,
long-range force-chain order, resonance locking, occupancy ordering, and actualization ordering;
(4) identify states that are physically possible but never intentionally targeted; (5) rank by
detectability / reversibility / stability / energy cost; (6) determine whether resonance could move a
material between organization states without major material alteration. **Success criterion:**
decide A) no unknown states, B) plausible but unexplored states, or C) strong AT-specific
candidates. Hostile audit. No archaeology. No ancient-civilization assumptions. Only physically
plausible organization states. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

## 1. Inventory — known organization states (organization only, chemistry ignored)

| State | Organization content (what is ordered) | Deliberately created by humanity? |
|---|---|---|
| **crystal** | periodic positional/lattice order | YES — single crystals, epitaxy |
| **glass** | frozen disorder (structural arrest, no long-range order) | YES — melt-quench, vapor deposition |
| **liquid** | mobile, isotropic, no long-range order | YES — trivial (melting) |
| **granular / jammed** | contact network + force-chain fabric | YES — packings, compaction, jamming |
| **phononic / photonic** | engineered periodicity of the MODULUS (band gaps) | YES — phononic crystals, metamaterials |
| **metamaterial** | engineered effective response (negative index, auxetic) | YES — manufactured structures |
| **topological** | order in the band topology (edge states) | YES — topological insulators, phononic TIs |
| **force-chain** | filamentary load-bearing network in a granular assembly | YES — photoelastic studies, DEM design |

**Every listed state is known and has been deliberately made.** The inventory of *organization
classes* is not empty of human intent: each of the eight has an active engineering discipline behind
it. This immediately weakens the naive claim that humanity has "never attempted" any of them.

**There ARE physically allowed organization states that humanity has not deliberately engineered** —
but they are *combinations and scales* of known ordering axes (long-range force-chain lattices,
phononic structure in jammed contacts, driven persistent jammed order, granular-topological
hybrids), not states that require AT's primitives to exist. The one candidate that is "strongly
AT-specific" (an actualization-ordering phase) has no measurable signature separable from known
physics.

## 4. Ranking — detectability, reversibility, stability, energy cost

Score each axis 1 (poor) to 5 (good). Reversibility below the damage threshold (NP_155/156);
stability limited by dissipation/relaxation; energy cost ~ power density I_c = σ_c²/(2ρc) ≈ 1–5 W/cm²
for yield-channel effects (NP_143).

| Candidate state | Detectability | Reversibility | Stability | Energy cost | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| long-range-ordered force-chain lattice | 4 (photoelasticity, X-ray/neutron, ultrasonic) | 3 (sub-damage reversible) | 2 (chain relaxation) | 3 (moderate drive + preload) | **12** |
| phononic structure in contact network | 3 (ultrasonic band-gap probes) | 3 | 2 | 3 | **11** |
| driven persistent jammed order | 3 | 2 (needs continuous drive) | 1 (dissipates on drive-off) | 4 (continuous power) | **10** |
| granular–topological hybrid | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 (precision fabrication) | **10** |
| coherently-softened fixed-T dial | 5 (modulus probe, NP_135) | 5 (re-lock) | 3 | 4 | **17** (already the best-studied; not "unknown") |

**Ranking:** the most accessible "unknown" states are the force-chain lattice and contact-network
phononic states (detectable with existing ultrasonic/photoelastic tools); the least accessible are
driven persistent phases (need continuous power) and granular–topological hybrids (precision).
Notably, the highest-scoring state (fixed-T coherent dial) is NOT unknown — it is the NP_132/140
state that materials science already partially explores.

## 5. Can resonance move a material between organization states without major material alteration?

**YES, in the sub-damage regime — and this is already the NP_155/156 conclusion.** Resonance
(preload + oriented vibration + frequency sweep) reconfigures contact networks and force chains
reversibly below the damage threshold; above it, reorganization proceeds by microcracking
(irreversible, though chemistry is unchanged). The move between jammed/metastable force-chain states
is known granular physics (memory, aging, cyclic-shear training — NP_158/159/163). No major material
alteration (no chemistry change) is required to traverse the organization state space. What resonance
CANNOT do (per NP_140) is reach a large fixed-T R→0 gel; the fixed-T elastic dial is small.

> network, a persistently driven jammed order, granular–topological hybrids), not states that require

> AT-specific candidate — a phase ordered only in occupancy/actualization variables — has no
> observable signature separable from ordinary ordered matter, so it is SPECULATIVE, not a discovery.
> Resonance CAN move a material between organization states without chemistry change, reversibly in
> the sub-damage regime (NP_155/156/132) — this is KNOWN physics, and its magnitude is bounded
> (NP_140: fixed-T elastic dial small). Determination: B (plausible but unexplored states EXIST) —

> AT-specific candidates) REFUTED as "strong" (SPECULATIVE at best, no independent signature);
> A (no unknown states) REFUTED. Classification: known organization-state inventory CORRESPONDENCE;

> *Proof sketch.* (1) Inventory known states (Section 1 — all eight deliberately engineered).

| "an actualization-ordering phase is a new state of matter" | no observable separates it from ordinary ordered matter — it is a re-label (NP_151), hence SPECULATIVE |
| "resonance can freely re-route organization" | only in the sub-damage regime; above it reorganization = microcracking (NP_155); fixed-T elastic change is small (NP_140) |
| "these states require AT to be predicted" | all identified candidates are expressible in known granular/topological/phononic terms |

**Conclusion.** There ARE physically plausible organization states humanity has never deliberately
engineered — long-range-ordered force-chain lattices, phononic structure in jammed contact networks,
persistently driven jammed order, and granular–topological hybrids — but every one is a combination
or a scale of known ordering physics, expressible in ordinary condensed-matter terms. The
AT-specific framing (organization of the Difference-Network as primary) does not predict a new state
with an independent observable; it re-labels the known defect/contact/coherence state space
(NP_151/157). Resonance can move a material between organization states without changing chemistry,
reversibly below the damage threshold (NP_155/156) and within the magnitude bound of the fixed-T dial
(NP_140). Success criterion: **B — plausible but unexplored states exist; C is not met as "strong"**
(the AT-only candidates are speculative). No new primitive; canonical AT unchanged.
