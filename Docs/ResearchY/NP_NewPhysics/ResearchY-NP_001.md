## 3. Top-10 V2.2 targets

### T1 — Measurement Origin (the deepest next target)

- **Expected payoff:** complete the QM measurement chain — the two-quadrature
  reconstruction basis (D_037) + state identity (D_039) + collapse (QG73) into a single
  derived measurement formalism.
- **Required derivations:** measurement = information-extraction from the complex state;
  the Born rule as the reconstruction fidelity (Σ|ψ|² = 1 already exact); the collapse
  event as an information-theoretic projection.
- **Possible predictions:** measurement-disturbance relations from the two-quadrature
  basis; a derived bound on simultaneous (magnitude, phase) knowledge.

### T2 — Information-Encoded Measurement

- **Expected payoff:** unify QG228 (I = KL(ρ‖uniform)) with D_039 (state identity =
  Difference applied) into a measurement-as-information theory.
- **Required derivations:** the observable state as the information record; measurement
  as Difference-readout; entropy change during measurement from the KL structure.
- **Possible predictions:** information-theoretic uncertainty; measurement entropy from
  the octave record.

### T3 — Baryon Asymmetry (antimatter)

- **Expected payoff:** explain matter–antimatter asymmetry from the phase/CP structure
  (CP derived, QG166) + the two-DOF complex state (D_036).
- **Required derivations:** the CP phase δ = asin(r) (QG166) driving a
  matter-antimatter asymmetry through the complex-state phase; a Sakharov-style count
  from the phase structure.
- **Possible predictions:** the baryon asymmetry magnitude η_B from the CP phase and
  the D96 octave structure.

### T4 — Neutrino Mass Hierarchy

- **Expected payoff:** complete the neutrino sector — masses (QG172) and Majorana
  (QG179) derived; the hierarchy/origin partially open; P2 0νββ pre-registered.
- **Required derivations:** the hierarchy pattern from the fermionic anchor m_e and the
  spectral ratios; the 0νββ amplitude from the Majorana channel (48/95 self-conjugate).
- **Possible predictions:** sharpening of P2 (m_ββ = 2.02 meV) — nEXO/LEGEND-1000
  reach; normal vs inverted ordering from the anchor structure.

### T5 — CMB Anisotropy Spectrum

- **Expected payoff:** extend n_s (QG237) and acoustic peaks (QG238) to the full CMB
  angular spectrum.
- **Required derivations:** the acoustic-peak hierarchy (ℓ₁ = 220.48, r₂₁, r₃₁) mapped
  onto the D96 octave rungs; the damping tail from the mode structure.
- **Possible predictions:** additional peak ratios and the tensor/scalar ratio from the
  octave ladder.

### T6 — Dark-Matter Particle Status

- **Expected payoff:** determine whether the deficit (QG195/206) admits a particle
  reading or is purely dynamical.
- **Required derivations:** the deficit dust as a field vs a particle; the mass scale of
  a dark candidate from the deficit density (Ωm − Ωbaryonic).
- **Possible predictions:** the dark-matter mass scale from the deficit structure;
  interaction cross-section bounds.

### T7 — Two-Anchor Ratio (v/m_e)

- **Expected payoff:** test whether v/m_e ≈ 5e5 is reducible despite D_013/D_044
  (boundary) — using the new origin-chain tools.
- **Required derivations:** search for a canonical construction of v/m_e from the
  derived chain (span, ratios, octaves); if none, strengthen the boundary.
- **Possible predictions:** a derived relation would predict m_e from v (or vice versa)
  at fixed precision; otherwise the boundary is confirmed with high confidence.

### T8 — Λ Coincidence

- **Expected payoff:** explain the coincidence Λ ~ H² ~ ρ̄ as a structural identity
  (already derived, QG230) and quantify why the present epoch is special.
- **Required derivations:** the conformal scale a = ρ^(1/d) evolution vs the vacuum
  fraction ΩΛ; the present-epoch timing from the octave record.
- **Possible predictions:** ΩΛ(t) evolution; a preferred epoch from the density record.

### T9 — Collapse / Observer

- **Expected payoff:** resolve the collapse binary (QG73) using the measurement basis
  (D_037) and the observer as an information-extraction entity.
- **Required derivations:** collapse as the Difference-readout of the complex state;
  the observer's role as the reconstructor (D_037: z = a + ib).
- **Possible predictions:** a derived no-cloning/no-deletion from the two-quadrature
  basis; observer-dependent phase relations.

### T10 — Cosmological Initial Conditions

- **Expected payoff:** extend QG227 (uniform critical state) to the perturbation origin
  (Poisson variance seeds, QG231 already derived).
- **Required derivations:** the initial perturbation spectrum from the counting
  variance; the scale-free spectrum without inflation.
- **Possible predictions:** the primordial spectral index n_s from the octave structure
  (already 0.96497, QG237) — sharpened with the V2.1 tools.
