## Purpose

is an industrial technology." NP_151 asks the honesty question: **which conclusions actually require
Actualization Theory?** Program: (1) inventory all NP_131–150 results; (2) classify each as KNOWN
PHYSICS / AT INTERPRETATION / AT PREDICTION / AT UNIQUE PREDICTION; (3) remove AT completely and see
what survives; (4) identify what would NOT have been obtained from standard condensed-matter physics.
**Success criterion:** a clean separation between known physics, AT reinterpretation, and genuinely new
AT content. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.
