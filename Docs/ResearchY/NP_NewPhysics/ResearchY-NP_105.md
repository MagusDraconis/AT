## Purpose

NP_104 established that Difference cannot cease. NP_105 asks the conservation question those
results make unavoidable: **is Difference the true conserved quantity of the theory — the parent
law from which every other conservation law projects?** Program: (1) inventory every conservation
law; (2) remove Difference and see what breaks; (3) compare conservation of count vs conservation
of Difference; (4) test whether every conservation law is a projection of Difference conservation;
(5) determine A/B/C/D; (6) search for the Noether / symmetry / counting analogue. **Success
criterion:** determine whether the deepest conservation principle is Conservation of Difference or
something deeper. No new primitives; canonical AT unchanged.
