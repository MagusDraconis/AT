## Purpose

NP_100 established binding = resonance locking (deficit clustering held by persistent generator
action). NP_101 asks the scale question those results leave open: **why do stable structures
appear at many different scales — particle, atom, molecule, crystal, planet, galaxy?** Program:
(1) inventory every stable structure; (2) find what is common across all scales; (3) test whether
stability always means resonance locking / phase synchronization / deficit clustering /
generator-balanced fixed points; (4) determine whether hierarchical structure is derived /
emergent / boundary; (5) search for a universal stability principle. **Success criterion:** explain
why reality naturally forms nested structures across scales. No new primitives; canonical AT
unchanged.
