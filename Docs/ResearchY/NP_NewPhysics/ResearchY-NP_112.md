## Purpose

NP_111 established the observer = a persistent Difference structure that actualizes distinctions.
NP_112 asks the experiential question that follows: **why does a persistent observer experience a
stable, continuous reality?** Program: (1) trace Difference → observer → observation → reality; (2)
determine whether reality is a sequence of actualizations / integrated observations / persistent
structure recognition / emergent narrative; (3) test why a stable observer sees a stable world; (4)
compare single / repeated / integrated actualizations; (5) determine whether reality is
observer-independent / observer-relative / structure-relative. **Success criterion:** explain the
appearance of a stable, continuous reality from the already-derived ontology. No new primitives;
canonical AT unchanged.


## 3. Why a stable observer sees a stable world

**Two stabilities multiply:**

1. **The world is stable.** Persistent structures (inertia NP_094, binding NP_100, hierarchy NP_101)
   are stable fixed points of actualization; decoherence (NP_099) collapses each wave packet to a
   single non-interfering classical worldline. The world's *structures* persist and its *paths* are
   definite.
2. **The observer is stable.** It is itself a persistent structure (a bound hierarchy, NP_111), so
   it is the *same reader* across ticks.

**Repeated readings therefore agree.** Each tick the observer actualizes the same persistent
structure (the world), decohered to a definite state — so the observer's successive observations are
*consistent*, and the world appears stable and continuous. Stability of experience =
(persistence of the reader) × (persistence of the read).

## 5. Observer-independent / observer-relative / structure-relative?

| Reading | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **observer-independent** | **PARTIAL.** The persistent structures exist whether or not read (states pre-exist measurement, M_005) — but "reality" is always *experienced* by a reader. |
| **observer-relative** | **REFUTED.** The world's structures do not depend on any particular observer — they are real persistent Differences. |
| **structure-relative** | **YES — the answer.** Reality is the relation between persistent structures: a persistent structure (the observer) reading other persistent structures (the world). It is relative to *structure*, not to a particular observer. |

**Reality is STRUCTURE-RELATIVE** — neither a view from nowhere (observer-independent) nor a private
illusion (observer-relative). Real = persistent Difference structures; any persistent structure can
read them, and the stable appearance is the joint persistence of reader and read.
