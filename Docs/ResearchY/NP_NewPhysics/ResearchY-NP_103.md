## Purpose

NP_102 established existence = Difference + Persistence. NP_103 asks the complement: **what ceases
to exist, and how?** Program: (1) define NOT(Existence); (2) test loss of Difference / Persistence /
Localization / Binding; (3) trace particle decay, decoherence, dissociation, thermalization; (4)
determine when a thing stops existing; (5) compare stable / unstable / transient; (6) determine
whether information is destroyed / redistributed / preserved; (7) locate the final stage (structure
→ pattern → noise → ??). **Success criterion:** the AT ontology of nonexistence and decay. No new
primitives; canonical AT unchanged.

## 1. Define NOT(Existence)

From NP_102:

```
EXISTENCE  =  DIFFERENCE (distinguishable)  ∧  PERSISTENCE (stable)
NOT(Existence)  =  ¬Difference  ∨  ¬Persistence
```

A thing ceases to exist when it loses **either** its distinguishability **or** its persistence.
These are the two decay channels:

| Channel | What is lost | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **¬Persistence** (decay) | stability — the structure dissolves | particle decay, dissociation |
| **¬Difference** (merging) | distinguishability — the identity merges into the background | thermalization into noise |

---

## 2. Loss of A / B / C / D

| Lost | Does the thing cease to exist? |
|---|---|
| **A) Difference (distinguishability)** | **YES.** Losing its identity (merging into the uniform bath) is full cessation. |
| **B) Persistence (stability)** | **YES.** Losing stability (decay/dissolution) is full cessation. |
| **C) Localization** | **PARTIAL.** A wave packet spreading loses its *localization*, but the resonance persists (delocalized) — the thing changes form, it does not vanish. |
| **D) Binding** | **PARTIAL.** A bound structure unlocking (dissociation) ends the *composite*, but its constituents persist. |

**Only the loss of Difference (A) or Persistence (B) is full nonexistence** — the two conditions of
existence (NP_102). Loss of localization (C) or binding (D) changes the form, not the underlying
existence.

## 3. Trace decay, decoherence, dissociation, thermalization

| Process | What happens | What ceases to exist |
|---|---|---|
| **particle decay** | a resonance mode transitions to lower modes (NP_075) | the original particle's resonance class (its ω₀/k) |
| **decoherence** | a wave packet loses phase coherence (NP_099) | the *quantum* superposition; the classical object continues |
| **dissociation** | a bound structure unlocks (NP_100) | the *composite*; the constituents are released |
| **thermalization** | heat spreads the count into uniform occupancy (NP_096) | the *structure's* distinguishability (its identity) |

Each process redistributes the conserved count (energy, NP_081) — nothing is created or destroyed;
the *structure* or *identity* ceases, the *content* is redistributed.

---

## 4. When does a thing stop existing?

| Loss | Full cessation? |
|---|---|
| its **resonance** disappears (decay) | **YES** — the thing's identity (resonance class) is gone |
| its **localization** disappears (decoherence) | **PARTIAL** — the quantum thing ceases, the classical continues |
| its **binding** disappears (dissociation) | **PARTIAL** — the composite ceases, the constituents persist |
| its **distinguishability** disappears (thermalization) | **YES** — the thing merges into the uniform bath; its identity is erased |

**A thing fully ceases to exist when its resonance (identity) or its distinguishability
disappears.** Localization and binding are form, not existence.

---

## 5. Stable / unstable / transient

| Object | Persistence | Existence |
|---|---|---|
| **stable object** | persists indefinitely | EXISTS (Difference + Persistence) |
| **unstable object** | decays after finite ticks | EXISTS for a finite time, then ceases |
| **transient fluctuation** | never persists | NEVER EXISTS as a thing (a momentary actualization) |

## 6. Is information destroyed, redistributed, or preserved?

| Quantity | Fate |
|---|---|
| the count (energy) | **CONSERVED** (Σρ = 1, NP_081/QG216) |
| the distinguishability (95 states) | **PRESERVED** (M_005 — reveal + redistribute, not create/destroy) |
| the occupancy information I_occ = ln K − H | **REDISTRIBUTED toward zero** (NP_096) as the count spreads into uniform noise |

**Information is REDISTRIBUTED, not destroyed.** The conserved count spreads into more modes
(higher entropy); the distinguishability is preserved in total but spread out into random phase.
"Erasure" (I_occ → 0) is the *spreading*, not the destruction.

## 7. The final stage — structure → pattern → noise → ??

```
structure  (bound, locked, distinguishable)          [existence]
   →  pattern   (a coherent wave packet)              [transient, still distinguishable]
   →  noise     (random phase, uniform occupancy)     [distinguishability erased]
   →  UNIFORM   (ρ_k = 1/K, maximum entropy)          [the equilibrium / heat death]
   →  DIFFERENCE (the network of distinctions)        [the substrate that never ceases]
```

The chain terminates at **uniform** (maximum entropy, ρ_k = 1/K, the QG227 reference state), beyond
which only **Difference** remains — the primitive substrate, which cannot cease because it is the
ground of existence itself. Nonexistence never reaches "nothing": it reaches uniform noise over the
irreducible Difference.

## 8. Counterexamples

| Attempt | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "nonexistence reaches nothing" | the substrate Difference always remains; decay reaches uniform noise, not nothing |
| "information is destroyed" | count is conserved (Σρ = 1); information is redistributed, not destroyed (M_005) |
| "loss of localization = nonexistence" | a spread packet is still a resonance — it changes form, it does not vanish |
| "loss of binding = nonexistence" | dissociation ends the composite, but the constituents persist |
| "a transient fluctuation exists" | it never persists — it is a momentary actualization, not a thing |

## 9. Falsification paths

| Claim | Falsification |
|---|---|
| nonexistence = ¬Difference ∨ ¬Persistence | a thing that ceases to exist without losing distinguishability or persistence |

| the final stage is uniform, not nothing | a decay that reaches a state with no Difference substrate |
| loss of localization/binding is partial | a spread packet or dissociated composite that fully ceases (constituents gone) |
