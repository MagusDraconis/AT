## Purpose

NP_088 established the D96 ⊗ D96 ⊗ D96 network already produces emergent 3D geometry; NP_089 showed
its rotational symmetry is only approximate. NP_090 asks the ontological question those two leave
open: **what physically IS the D96 network — what are the nodes, what are the links, and what
propagates?** Program: (1) inventory every occurrence of D96 / tensor products / coupled rings /
network structure; (2) determine what a node and a link are; (3) test A/B/C/D; (4) determine
whether particles are modes of one D96 or the whole network; (5) determine whether geometry is
embedded or emergent from connectivity; (6) construct the minimal visualization. **Success
criterion:** provide the canonical ontology — what exists, what connects, what propagates. No new
primitives; canonical AT unchanged.


## 4. Are particles modes of one D96, or of the whole network?

**Modes of ONE D96 (the seed ring).** The elementary particles are the resonance classes of a
single ring's spectrum (NP_072/073): the electron, muon, tau, and the quarks are the stable
bottom-modes of the three octave bands of ONE 96-site ring. The **tensor-product network**
(D96 ⊗ D96 ⊗ D96) is not a larger set of particles — it supplies the **spatial geometry** (3D, p = 3)
on which those particles propagate. One ring gives the particles; the network gives the space.

which the network is placed; it is the trace face of the difference structure itself, and ψ (the
traceless face) is its curvature. The dimension d = 3 is DERIVED (QG197), and the 3D network
