# TRM Legacy Module Prioritization Audit

**Goal:** rank the five missing TRM modules (Theta Chain, Memory Channel, m=3
Closure, Frame Dragging, Unified Action) by theoretical value for THE Q-MODEL.
**Discipline:** no physics invented; every row traces to `TRM_Reconciliation_Audit.md`
and the `Coverage_Report.md` / `TQM_Encyclopedia.md` status table.

---

## 1. Method

Seven criteria per module: (1) existing overlap with TQM, (2) new mathematics,
(3) dependency on other modules, (4) testability, (5) expected encyclopedia
impact, (6) chance of being mere old wording, (7) chance of genuinely new
physics. Priority = theoretical value (impact × newness × testability) ÷
(dependency × old-wording risk).

### 2.1 m=3 Closure — the standout

TRM gives a *concrete, testable* alternative to TQM's multiplicity route to the
integer 3: rational mode-locking $\Omega=(q+3)/q$, $\gamma=1/\Omega\approx0.84$–$0.86$.
This is **not** a re-wording of Phases 150–151 ($N\ge3$ derived, $N\le3$ drawn) —
it is a different mechanism that produces *numbers* (γ, Ω). It is self-contained,
and its predictions can be checked against the charged-lepton ratios / Koide angle,
which are TQM's own open problem (QG-047). This is the only module that both
**addresses the central "recurring 3" mystery** and is **immediately falsifiable**.

### 2.2 Frame Dragging — highest-risk/high-reward

The vector sector $\vec A_T,\ \vec B_T=\nabla\times\vec A_T$ is the one place TRM
could *extend* TQM beyond scalar phase-gradient gravity (QG-022). Its observable
(Lense–Thirring precession) is well-defined. But it risks **contradicting**
TQM's established scalar-only gravity and its zero-free-parameter RAR win
($g_\dagger=cH_0/2\pi$), so it must be framed as an *extension*, not a replacement.

### 2.3 Memory Channel — concrete but niche

The invariant $I\propto\phi^2\lvert\dot\mu\rvert$ (MC09–12) is genuine new
mathematics with no TQM counterpart, and is self-contained. Its weakness is
**no direct observable** and unclear physical interpretation — it cannot be
prioritized above the two testable candidates.

### 2.4 Theta Chain — deprioritized

$\Theta\to O_5\to\lambda_\Theta\to g_{\rm obs}$ collides with TQM's information-layer
Θ (TQM-128–133). Its guarded prefixes (TO/TQK/LC/TOL) and opaque "observable
extraction to $g_{\rm obs}$" make it hard to test, and it depends on the gauge-like
Θ sector existing at all. Highest old-wording risk.

### 2.5 Unified Action — capstone, not a starting point

$S_{\rm eff}[T,\vec A_T,\Theta]$ is a **roadmap** (UF01–09; TRM itself disclaims
closure). It depends on every other module being defined first, so it cannot be
prioritized until 2.1–2.4 are settled.

## 5. Conclusion

**Priority 1 — m=3 Closure.** It is the highest-value module because it attacks
TQM's central open mystery (why 3) with a *different*, *self-contained*, and
*immediately falsifiable* mechanism (γ≈0.84–0.86), and it is the least likely to
be old wording. **Priority 2 — Frame Dragging**, the only candidate to extend TQM
beyond scalar gravity, testable but requiring care not to contradict QG-022.
**Priority 3 — Memory Channel**, genuine but untestable-in-itself mathematics.
Theta Chain and Unified Action are deprioritized (homonym/roadmap risk).
