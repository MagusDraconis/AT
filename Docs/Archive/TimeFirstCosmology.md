# Time-First Cosmology (QG-081)

A "model-free first" reconstruction that treats time evolution as fundamental and spatial
expansion as emergent (i.e., derived, not assumed).

## Axioms (only direct observables)
1. Redshift `1+z` is a frequency ratio (observed).
2. Time dilation factor is `(1+z)` (observed, b=1).
3. Angular scales θ and fluxes are measured (observed).
4. The CMB is a `T0 = 2.725 K` blackbody (observed).

## Reconstruction
- Define a physical clock `τ` with rate `γ(t) = dτ/dt`. Redshift forces `1+z = γ_obs/γ_emit`.
- Time dilation forces the same factor `γ_obs/γ_emit = (1+z)`, i.e. `γ` must scale like `a`.
- With `γ(t) = a(t)`, physical time is conformal time `η = ∫a dt`, and the reconstruction is
  FLRW in conformal coordinates — the SAME metric, read as evolving time instead of space.

## Consequences
- All FLRW distances (D_L, D_A, χ) are reproduced; BAO, CMB acoustic scale, SN Ia all pass.
- `g† = c·d(ln γ)/dt/2π = cH/2π`: the RAR acceleration scale is the cosmic clock's
  log-acceleration — a natural "time-first" statement of AT's relation.
- "Dark energy" becomes "clock acceleration" (τ̈/τ̇ > 0); ΩΛ is no longer a substance, but a
  property of the time axis.

## What does NOT change
Nothing observable. The reconstruction is observationally equivalent to ΛCDM (QG-080):
it relabels `a(t)` → `γ(t)` but makes no new prediction. Time-first cosmology is a powerful
conceptual reframing, not a competing theory.

## Hostile-science conclusion
"Was it measured, or inferred?" — Redshift, dilation, flux, angle, blackbody: **measured**.
Scale factor, H(z), distances, ΩΛ, dark energy: **inferred** after assuming FLRW. The
inference can be replaced by evolving time without changing any observation — so the claim
"space physically expands" is one valid interpretation among observationally-equivalent ones.
