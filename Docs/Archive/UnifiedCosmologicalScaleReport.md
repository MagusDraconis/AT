# Unified Cosmological Scale Report (QG-095)

Are Λ ~ 1/√N and g† independent anomalies, or manifestations of one scale?

## The single cosmic rate H
Both surviving anchors are powers of the SAME rate H:

- **Λ** (causal set): Λ·l_P² = (H·l_P/c)² ⇒ **Λ ~ H²/c²** (the 'why now' relation, with α = O(1)).
- **g†** (time scale): **g† = cH/2π** (with the coincidental 2π).

So Λ and g† are NOT independent — they are dimensional consequences of the one rate H. But this
is a dimensional link, not a deep unification.

## Why it is not a deep unification
- Λ → H gives only the magnitude of H (H ~ c√Λ), not its Ωm/ΩΛ time-dependence.
- g† → Λ requires H = 2π g†/c, which assumes the 2π coincidence (circular).
- Λ → g† requires g† ~ c²√Λ/2π — again the 2π.

Neither derives the other without re-introducing the 2π (for g†) or α (for Λ). They are linked
only through H, and only dimensionally.

## Tuning
- Λ ~ H²/c² : α = 2.07 (O(1), derived, no tuning — QG-093).
- g† ~ cH/2π : the 2π is a 'nice-number accident' (~30% coincidence — QG-085).

The unification does NOT rescue g† from its 2π problem.

## Verdict
Levels 1–4 pass; Level 5 fails. Λ and g† share the common rate H (common cosmological/causal-set
origin), but no new falsifiable prediction emerges beyond the existing g†(z) evolution test.
