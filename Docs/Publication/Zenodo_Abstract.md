The Actualization Theory (AT), formerly THE Q-MODEL, reconstructs physics from the primitive
**Difference** and the tensor reference η through the hierarchy **Difference → Actualization →
Inevitable Spectrum → Physics**, with the D96 spectrum as the source of quantum mechanics, gravity,
standard-model observables and cosmology.

Its central claim is the **structure/content split**: the *form* of observable structure — topology,
symmetry, distribution shape, lower bounds — is provable from the primitives, while the *content*
— masses, couplings, multiplicities, angles — is a contingent draw, **classified rather than
derived**.

**V2.1** closed the origin chain end to end, leaving exactly **five irreducible boundaries**:
{Difference, η}, the Z2-paired (complex) sector, the three octave families, {SU(2) gauge, j = 1/2},
and {v, m_e}.

**V2.2** is the **New Physics** program: 172 audits (NP_001–NP_175). It established that **no
AT-specific observable remains** — the predicted signature is generic across the controls — and
therefore **formally closed** the lock-lattice physical-realizability program (NP_175, 2026-09-11).
In parallel it extended the gravity/time audits (G_021–G_036) and opened an **electromagnetism**
group (E_001). The headline results of that extension are deliberately negative, and are recorded
in full rather than summarised away:

- **The derived spatial structure is excluded by measurement.** AT derives its spatial sector, and
  the derived form is conformal, giving **γ = −1 exactly** — zero light bending and zero Shapiro
  delay — refuted by **Cassini at 8.6957×10⁴ σ**.
- **The spatial sector cannot be derived.** A no-go theorem (G_030) establishes that no local
  `B(r) = F(σ)` satisfies the Newton limit, Cassini, light deflection, the clock sector and the
  no-new-primitive rule. It closes instead on the **postulate** **`g_rr = 2 − ρ^(2/d)`** (G_029),
  which reproduces **γ = +1** and is **non-conformal** (A ≠ B).
- **The electromagnetic dynamics is declared but never computed.** The Noether currents,
  `F^a_μν`, `−¼F²` and the full Lagrangian density exist as **members returning strings** (E_001);
  the **sourced Maxwell equation** `∂_μF^μν = J^ν` is absent, as is any massless spin-1 wave
  equation, and the fine-structure constant carries **two contradictory values**.

**One result is a resolution.** The measured deflection of light is `((1+γ)/2)·4GM/(c²b)`, so the
spatial half is required: a time-dilation-only explanation gives **exactly half** the observed
bending and is excluded at **43,479 σ**. AT's surviving postulate reproduces the measured γ = +1
while differing from general relativity only at O(x²) — invisible in the solar system
(−2.7×10⁻¹¹ at solar compactness) but **−29.7 %** at neutron-star compactness.

The theory's **time sector stands on its own**: of 37 group-G results, **25 need g₀₀ and nothing
else**, the clock law `dτ/dt = ρ^(1/d)` and the source law `a = −(1/d)∇ln ρ = −∇A` are one

discriminating arena is neutron stars only and is currently limited by compactness precision
