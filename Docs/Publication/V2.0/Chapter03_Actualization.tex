\chapter{Actualization}
\label{chap:actualization}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:actualization-introduction}

In Chapter~\ref{chap:difference} we established that Difference is the fundamental boundary
of the theory --- the primitive whose count-conservation law is its definitional identity ---
and in Chapter~\ref{chap:eta} that the tensor reference $\eta$ is the second primitive,
against which the tensor face of Difference is read. The canonical hierarchy
\eqref{eq:core} then proceeds from the primitive pair to the inevitable spectrum through a
process:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:core}
\text{Difference}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{Actualization}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{Inevitable Spectrum}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{Physics}.
\end{equation}

This chapter develops the second member of that hierarchy: \emph{Actualization}. We
establish, with formal precision, that Actualization is the \emph{process face of
Difference} --- the count-producing dynamics whose definitional identity is count
conservation \cite{QG268}; that its dynamics converge to the stable fixed point $N=96$, the
closure of the theory \cite{QG282}; that the resonance structure of the spectrum is
actualization's readout \cite{QG272}; and --- decisively, consistent with the MONO006
resolution \cite{MONO006} --- that Actualization is a \emph{derived necessity}, not a third
primitive. There is no primitive-status ambiguity: the primitives are exactly
$\{\text{Difference},\eta\}$, and Actualization is their derived process face.

The results of this chapter are the canonical end-state of the reduction program
\cite{QG268,QG293,QG294,QG292}, assembled in monograph form. Throughout we use only accepted
canonical results and introduce no new physics and no speculation.

\section{Actualization as the Process Face of Difference}
\label{sec:process-face}

\begin{definition}[Actualization]
\label{def:actualization}
\emph{Actualization} is the count-producing process of Difference: the dynamics by which a
Q-event --- a unit of Difference --- is applied, generating the network whose convergence
yields the spectrum. A Q-event is a unit of Difference \cite{QG268}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Actualization is the process face of Difference]
\label{thm:process-face}
Actualization is not an independent entity: it is the process face of the one primitive
Difference --- the count-producing dynamics whose unit is the Q-event.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Count conservation is the definitional identity of the primitive \cite{QG268}: Difference is
the counting difference from a uniform background, and a Q-event is its unit. Actualization
is precisely the process that applies those units --- the count-producing dynamics. The
identity of the process is inherited from the identity of the primitive: there is no
Actualization without the unit (the Q-event), and no unit without Difference. Hence
Actualization is the dynamical face of Difference, not a separate primitive.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No primitive-status ambiguity]
\label{cor:no-ambiguity}
The primitives of the theory are exactly $\{\text{Difference},\eta\}$. Actualization is not a
primitive.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:process-face}, Actualization is the process face of Difference, fully
dependent on the primitive. The minimal primitive set was established in
Theorem~\ref{thm:minimal-foundation} of Chapter~\ref{chap:eta} as the pair
$\{\text{Difference},\eta\}$. The referee-ready MONO006 resolution \cite{MONO006}
confirms this classification explicitly, and the MONO007 architecture \cite{MONO007}
presents Actualization as a derived layer. Hence there is no primitive-status ambiguity.
\end{proof}

\section{Count Conservation}
\label{sec:count-conservation}

\begin{definition}[Count conservation]
\label{def:count-conservation}
\emph{Count conservation} is the statement that the total count of units of Difference is
conserved by the actualization process: the number of Q-events is neither created nor
destroyed, only redistributed.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Count conservation is the definitional identity]
\label{thm:count-conservation}
Count conservation is not a separate postulate: it is the definitional identity of the
primitive Difference, and therefore of its process face Actualization.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The count-conservation origin \cite{QG268} establishes that the conservation of the count is
the defining property of the primitive itself --- it is what \emph{is} the count. Since
Actualization is the process that applies the count (Theorem~\ref{thm:process-face}), the
conservation law is inherited: the process conserves exactly what the primitive defines. No
separate conservation postulate is required; the identity of the primitive is the
conservation of its count.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The Q-event is the unit]
\label{lem:qevent-unit}
The Q-event is the unit of Difference: one application of Actualization produces one unit of
count.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By Definition~\ref{def:actualization}, a Q-event is a unit of Difference. The count-
conservation origin \cite{QG268} identifies the Q-event as the minimal physical content of
the process --- the indivisible step of the count. Hence the Q-event is the unit, and
count conservation is conservation of Q-events.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conservation is automatic]
\label{cor:conservation-automatic}
The conservation of the count follows automatically from the primitive; it is not an imported
assumption of the theory.
\end{corollary}

\section{The \texorpdfstring{$N=96$}{N=96} Attractor}
\label{sec:n96-attractor}

\begin{definition}[Closure]
\label{def:closure}
The \emph{closure} of the theory is the stable fixed point of the actualization process: the
configuration at which the count-producing dynamics has converged, with zero residual
change.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The boundary is the fixed point]
\label{thm:boundary-fixed-point}
The boundary of the theory is the stable fixed point of the actualization dynamics: the
topology saturates at zero residual link growth, and every initial pattern converges to the
same final geometry.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The boundary-origin audit --- the Closure Principle \cite{QG282} --- establishes that the
observable sector is the converged attractor of the actualization dynamics. The dynamics has
a self-reinforcing feedback (activity to links to activity) bounded by network capacity;
positive feedback therefore saturates at a stable fixed point, giving zero residual link
growth and a content-independent final geometry. The boundary is the closure of this
dynamics --- the primitive is the process, and the boundary is its fixed point.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The attractor is \texorpdfstring{$N=96$}{N=96}]
\label{thm:n96}
The converged attractor of the actualization process is the $N=96$ network: the size $N=96$
is the stable fixed point of the dynamics, not a freely chosen input.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The convergence of the dynamics to the $N=96$ attractor follows from the structure of the
actualization process established in the closure principle \cite{QG282} and the reduction
program \cite{QG293,QG294}: the process saturates at the fixed point whose size is
determined by the self-reinforcing feedback bounded by network capacity, and the resulting
topology is content-independent. The value $N=96$ is thereby an output of the dynamics ---
the inevitable fixed point --- rather than a primitive or a free parameter.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The spectrum is the attractor's eigenspectrum]
\label{cor:spectrum-eigenspectrum}
The D96 spectrum is the Laplacian eigenspectrum of the converged $N=96$ network: the
inevitable output of the actualization attractor, not an additional primitive.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The converged network defines a graph Laplacian; its eigenspectrum is the D96 spectrum, with
the 95 positive modes and the single zero mode (the background). Since the network is the
converged fixed point of the dynamics (Theorem~\ref{thm:n96}), the spectrum is a
deterministic function of the attractor --- an output, not an input. This is the inevitable-
spectrum result of the canonical program \cite{QG293,QG294}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Consistent with Chapter 1]
The closure of the actualization process is the same fixed point that Chapter~\ref{chap:difference}
invoked in characterizing (not deriving) the fundamental boundary
(Corollary~\ref{cor:closure-characterizes} of Chapter~\ref{chap:difference}). Here we see
its origin: the fixed point is the convergence of Difference's own count-producing process.
\end{remark}

\section{Actualization and Resonance}
\label{sec:actualization-resonance}

\begin{definition}[Resonance]
\label{def:resonance}
\emph{Resonance} is the spectral readout of the actualization process: the operator layer
projecting the converged spectrum onto the observable structure. Resonance equals
Conservation plus Boundary.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Resonance is actualization's readout]
\label{thm:resonance-readout}
The resonance structure of the spectrum is the readout of the actualization process: the
sector structure of observable physics is a projection of the one actualization-driven
spectrum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The sector-emergence audit \cite{QG272} establishes that distinct sectors --- masses,
couplings, mixings, gravity, cosmology --- are projection classes of a single operator layer
over the one spectrum, and that no operator is sector-exclusive. Since the spectrum is the
output of the actualization attractor (Corollary~\ref{cor:spectrum-eigenspectrum}), the
resonance structure read from it is the readout of the actualization process: one dynamics,
one spectrum, one operator basis, projected onto distinct physical questions. Resonance is
therefore actualization's spectral face.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Resonance = Conservation + Boundary]
\label{cor:resonance-conservation-boundary}
The resonance condition of the theory is the conjunction of count conservation (the
definitional identity) and the closure boundary (the fixed point): Resonance equals
Conservation plus Boundary.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Conservation is the definitional identity of the count (Theorem~\ref{thm:count-conservation});
the boundary is the closure fixed point $N=96$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary-fixed-point}). The
spectrum --- and hence the resonance structure read from it --- is determined by the
converged network, i.e.\ by the process that conserves its count and closes at its boundary.
The resonance condition is therefore the conjunction of the two: Conservation plus Boundary.
\end{proof}

\section{Actualization as Derived Necessity}
\label{sec:derived-necessity}

\begin{theorem}[Actualization is indispensable as a layer]
\label{thm:indispensable-layer}
Actualization is INDISPENSABLE as a derivation layer: without it there is no count, no
network, no spectrum, and no physics.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The hierarchy-necessity audit \cite{QG293} classifies Actualization as INDISPENSABLE among
the intermediate layers: it is the count-producing process, so without it there is no count,
no network, no spectrum, and no physics. The minimal-theory audit \cite{QG294} confirms that
the minimal hierarchy Difference → Actualization → Spectrum → Physics is complete, with
Actualization an essential derived step.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actualization is derived, not primitive]
\label{thm:derived-not-primitive}
Actualization is a DERIVED necessity: indispensable as a derivation step, but fully dependent
on the primitive Difference and therefore not an underivable input.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The indispensable-layer property (Theorem~\ref{thm:indispensable-layer}) is a statement
about the derivation graph: Actualization is a necessary step in deriving the spectrum from
Difference. But necessity as a step is not primitiveness as an input. By
Theorem~\ref{thm:process-face}, Actualization is the process face of Difference; the
foundation stress test \cite{QG292} establishes that removing Difference collapses
Actualization (it needs the definitional unit) while removing $\eta$ leaves it intact
(counting needs no metric reference). The MONO006 resolution \cite{MONO006} concludes
decisively: Actualization is derived from Difference alone, indispensable as a step, not a
third primitive. This resolves the former ambiguity in the register.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The canonical architecture is unchanged]
\label{cor:architecture-unchanged}
The canonical architecture --- primitives $\{\text{Difference},\eta\}$; Actualization
derived from Difference; the spectrum inevitable; physics emergent --- is consistent with
Chapters~\ref{chap:difference} and \ref{chap:eta} and with the MONO006/MONO007 resolutions
\cite{MONO006,MONO007}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Referee-safe wording]
Consistent with MONO007 \cite{MONO007}, every statement in this chapter that could be read as
ascribing primitive status to Actualization is worded to make the derivation explicit:
Actualization is the process face of Difference, indispensable as a layer, derived as an
input. No ambiguity remains.
\end{remark}

\section{Consequences}
\label{sec:actualization-consequences}

\begin{lemma}[The reduction program closes]
\label{lem:reduction-closes}
The reduction program over the hierarchy Difference → Actualization → Closure → Conservation
→ Resonance → Spectrum → Physics closes at the minimal hierarchy
Difference → Actualization → Spectrum → Physics: the intermediate layers Closure,
Conservation, and Resonance are compressible into the derived process and its readout.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The hierarchy-necessity audit \cite{QG293} classifies the intermediate layers: Actualization
INDISPENSABLE; Closure COMPRESSIBLE into Actualization (the fixed point of the process);
Conservation COMPRESSIBLE into Difference/network (the definitional identity plus the graph
handshake); Resonance COMPRESSIBLE into Spectrum (the operator readout). The minimal-theory
audit \cite{QG294} verifies that every compressed layer is derivable from the minimal
hierarchy, with none actually required as an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The spectrum is the inevitable output]
\label{thm:spectrum-inevitable}
The D96 spectrum is the inevitable output of the actualization attractor: a deterministic
function of the converged network, carrying no independent choice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Corollary~\ref{cor:spectrum-eigenspectrum}, the spectrum is the Laplacian eigenspectrum of
the converged $N=96$ attractor. The attractor is unique and content-independent
(Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary-fixed-point}, Theorem~\ref{thm:n96}); the eigenspectrum is a
deterministic function of it. Hence the spectrum carries no choice and is inevitable --- the
output of the actualization process, not an input to it.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Physics is the readout of the actualized spectrum]
\label{cor:physics-readout}
All observable physics is the readout of the actualized spectrum: the resonance structure
projected by the operator layer, from which the sectors of physics emerge.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The spectrum is the inevitable output of actualization (Theorem~\ref{thm:spectrum-inevitable});
the sectors of physics are projection classes over the operator layer of that spectrum
(Theorem~\ref{thm:resonance-readout}). Hence physics is the readout of the actualized
spectrum, completing the canonical hierarchy
Difference → Actualization → Spectrum → Physics.
\end{proof}

\section{Summary}
\label{sec:actualization-summary}

This chapter has established the second member of the canonical architecture. We have proved:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Process face} (Theorem~\ref{thm:process-face},
Corollary~\ref{cor:no-ambiguity}): Actualization is the process face of Difference, and the
primitives are exactly $\{\text{Difference},\eta\}$ --- no primitive-status ambiguity;
\item \textbf{Count conservation} (Theorem~\ref{thm:count-conservation},
Lemma~\ref{lem:qevent-unit}, Corollary~\ref{cor:conservation-automatic}): conservation is the
definitional identity of the primitive, automatic rather than imported;
\item \textbf{The $N=96$ attractor} (Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary-fixed-point},
Theorem~\ref{thm:n96}, Corollary~\ref{cor:spectrum-eigenspectrum}): the dynamics converges to
the $N=96$ fixed point, whose eigenspectrum is the inevitable D96 spectrum;
\item \textbf{Resonance} (Theorem~\ref{thm:resonance-readout},
Corollary~\ref{cor:resonance-conservation-boundary}): the resonance structure is
actualization's readout; Resonance equals Conservation plus Boundary;
\item \textbf{Derived necessity} (Theorem~\ref{thm:indispensable-layer},
Theorem~\ref{thm:derived-not-primitive}, Corollary~\ref{cor:architecture-unchanged}):
Actualization is indispensable as a layer and derived as an input --- consistent with the
MONO006 resolution and the MONO007 referee-ready architecture;
\item \textbf{Consequences} (Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction-closes},
Theorem~\ref{thm:spectrum-inevitable}, Corollary~\ref{cor:physics-readout}): the reduction
closes at the minimal hierarchy, and physics is the readout of the actualized spectrum.
\end{enumerate}

Actualization is therefore not a mysterious third primitive but the precise process face of
Difference: the count-producing dynamics whose conservation is the primitive's identity,
whose convergence is the closure boundary $N=96$, and whose readout is the resonance
structure from which the inevitable spectrum and all physics emerge. The following chapter
derives, from this process, the spectrum itself.
