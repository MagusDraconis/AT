# Causal Scale Unification (QG-096)

Can causal spacetime discreteness generate g† ~ 1e-10 m/s² the same way it generates Λ ~ 1/√N?

## What causal discreteness produces
- Λ ~ 1/√N: a COUNTING result (N = (R_H/l_P)⁴) → Λ ~ H²/c². Fully derived, 0 free params, α = O(1).
- g†: the causal-depth growth rate is d ln(depth)/dt = H (QG-091) → a_eff = cH. The MAGNITUDE
  (~6.5e-10) is natural — but the 1/(2π) that turns cH into a₀ is NOT generated.

## Why the 2π is the obstruction
Counting/order gives cH. The 2π is an angular-frequency (radians→cycles) factor, not a
counting/order quantity. None of the candidate derivations (causal-depth growth, Poisson
fluctuations, ever-present Λ, causal horizon) produces the 2π — all give cH.

## Λ vs g†
| quantity | free params | derivation | tuning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Λ ~ 1/√N | 0 | fully derived (counting) | α = O(1) |
| g† = cH/2π | 0 for cH, 2π inserted | half derived | 2π coincidental |

## Verdict
Levels 1–2, 4 pass; Level 3 (g† emerges) and Level 5 (falsifiable consequence) fail. Causal
discreteness generates the cH SCALE but NOT the 2π — so g† = cH/2π is HALF-predicted, and Λ and
g† are NOT unified. The 2π remains the same un-unified coincidence as in QG-085.
