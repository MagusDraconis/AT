This paper reports the closed form of the theory at version 1.0. It is a synthesis of
an extended hostile-audit program (Phases 1–159, the QG/X/DATA/QM research programs,
and the TRM legacy reconciliation), not a new derivation. Throughout, we distinguish
three categories with explicit confidence assessments, and we state the no-go
theorems that bound what the theory cannot do.
