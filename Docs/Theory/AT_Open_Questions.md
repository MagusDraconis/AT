# AT Open Questions

## Priority-Ordered Research Roadmap After AT-154

---

## Priority 1 — The Measurement Problem (Irreducible)

**Status**: UNSOLVED since 1926 (Born). Affects ALL formulations of QM.

**What we know**: Decoherence explains disappearance of interference. Pointer states explain which basis is stable. Born statistics appear on the diagonal.

**What we don't know**: Why ONE outcome occurs. The mechanism of wavefunction collapse. The "and" → "or" transition.

**AT angle**: Can the Q-network environment (large graph coupled to a small subsystem) provide new insights? Does the graph structure affect decoherence rates or pointer state selection?

---

## Priority 2 — Experimental Realization

**Status**: No physical experiment has been proposed or conducted.

**Key question**: Can Q-like topological charges be realized in physical systems?

**Candidate platforms**:
- Coupled oscillator arrays (mechanical or electrical)
- Superconducting qubit networks
- Cold atom optical lattices
- Photonic waveguide arrays
- Spin networks (NMR)

**Required**: A system with discrete nodes, local interactions, and measurable collective modes that can be compared with AT predictions.

---

## Priority 3 — Dynamic Graph Topology

**Status**: Theoretical possibility, not yet explored.

**Key question**: If Q charges can MOVE (changing the graph structure), does open-ended innovation become possible?

**Hypothesis**: Static graphs → finite eigenmode spectrum → saturated innovation. Dynamic graphs → evolving spectrum → potentially open-ended innovation (niche construction).

**Required**: Model moving Q charges. Study how L_Q(t) affects species, fitness, and innovation.

---

## Priority 4 — Higher-Dimensional Q Networks

**Status**: AT-143 tested up to 3D lattices. 2D/3D spectra computed but not exhaustively studied.

**Key question**: Do higher dimensions produce qualitatively new phenomena?

**Known**: 2D hexagonal → Dirac cones (graphene-like). 3D → richer spectra, more species.

**Unknown**: Topological edge states? Gapped vs gapless phases? Dimensional crossovers?

---

## Priority 5 — Nonlinear Q Interactions

**Status**: AT-141 tested nonlinear mode composition (product terms). Not essential for current species catalog.

**Key question**: Under what conditions do nonlinear Q interactions produce qualitatively new phenomena?

**Candidates**: Soliton-like modes, nonlinear eigenmodes, frequency combs, chaos.

---

## Priority 6 — Continuum Limit (Q → ∞)

**Status**: AT uses finite Q (discrete). Scaling laws derived for large Q.

**Key question**: What is the exact relationship between discrete Q-network physics and continuum field theory?

**Known**: L_Q → -∇² as Δx → 0. Eigenmodes → plane waves.

**Unknown**: How do species, fitness, and evolution behave in the continuum limit? Does the discrete structure leave imprints on continuum observables?

---

## Priority 7 — Born Rule Derivation

**Status**: Gleason's theorem provides uniqueness. Additivity is the assumption.

**Key question**: Can additivity be derived from Q-network structure?

**Approach**: Does the graph structure impose additivity constraints on measurement outcomes? Does the spectral decomposition of L_Q naturally single out the Born rule?

---

## Priority 8 — Thermodynamic Limit

**Status**: Not explored.

**Key question**: What are the thermodynamic properties of large Q-networks? Do phase transitions occur? Is there an equation of state?

---

## Priority 9 — Information-Theoretic Foundations

**Status**: Information capacity C = log₂(Q) established (AT-145).

**Key question**: Can information theory provide deeper foundations? Is the Born rule derivable from information-theoretic principles (max entropy, etc.)?

---

## Priority 10 — Comparison with Other Quantum Foundations

**Status**: Not yet done.

**Candidates for comparison**: Many-worlds, Bohmian mechanics, QBism, relational QM, consistent histories, objective collapse models.

**Key question**: Does AT's graph-theoretic foundation align with or contradict any existing interpretation?
