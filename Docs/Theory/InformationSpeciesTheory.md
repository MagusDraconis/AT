# Information Species Theory

## AT-133: The Emergence of an Information Ecology

### Abstract

The Θ field supports an information ecology — stable species of
information patterns that emerge through self-organization.
Four species are identified: Uniform, Standing Wave, Anti-Phase,
and Composite. This is the highest level of information structure
in proto-matter.
