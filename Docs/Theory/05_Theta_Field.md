# 5. Theta Field

## The Collective Phase Coherence of Q Ensembles

---

## Definition

The Theta field Θ(x,t) is the collective phase coherence of the Q charge ensemble:

**Θ(x,t) = ⟨θ_i(t)⟩_local** (local average of Q phases)

Status: DERIVED — emerges from Q interactions at ρ_Q > 0.3 (AT-128).

---

## Dynamics

Θ obeys damped wave dynamics:

**∂²Θ/∂t² + γ·∂Θ/∂t = c²·∂²Θ/∂x²**

where c² = 1/Δx² (from the graph Laplacian scaling).

**Discretized form**: Θ → vector ψ on N nodes, evolution: i∂ψ/∂t = L_Q ψ (Schrödinger form, Postulate 2).

**Stationary states**: ψ_k(t) = exp(-iλ_k t) · v_k where v_k are eigenvectors of L_Q.

---

## Autonomy

Θ becomes an autonomous field at ρ_Q > 0.3 (AT-128). Below this threshold, Θ is slaved to Q dynamics. Above it, Θ has its own independent dynamics — information can propagate, persist, and interact without direct Q involvement.
