## Fitness Law: w = r/c

**Statement**: The fitness of an information species is its resource efficiency — reproduction rate divided by resource consumption.

**Evidence** (AT-136): Spearman ρ = 1.000 against observed selection outcomes. Perfect rank prediction across all 4 species.

**Derivation**: From resource-constrained Lotka-Volterra: dN/dt = r·N·(1 - c·N/K). At equilibrium N* = K/c. Fitness ∝ r/c.

**Universality** (AT-137): Evolution persists across 7 alternative fitness models × 7 resource regimes (100% robustness). The specific fitness function is not invariant, but the PHENOMENON of selection is.

**Mapping** (AT-139): 13 attractor basins from 563/1000 converged initial conditions.
