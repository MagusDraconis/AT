# ResearchXB-001: Origin of Abundance

## Motivation

TQM-X065b discovered a fundamental split in physical questions:

| Category | Question | Mechanism | Success Rate |
|----------|----------|-----------|:--:|
| **Identity** | "What exists?" | Topology | **~93%** |
| **Abundance** | "How much exists?" | History | **~14%** |

ResearchX (X001-X065) investigated Identity Physics — deriving WHAT exists from Q-event topology.

ResearchXB investigates Abundance Physics — understanding WHY abundance quantities resist topological derivation and whether they share a common origin.

## The Two-Layer Ontology

### Layer 1: Identity (Topology → What Exists)
- Particles, gauge symmetries, generations
- Discrete properties determined by topological invariants
- Universal across all universes with the same primitives

### Layer 2: Abundance (History → How Much Exists)
- Masses, couplings, relic densities
- Continuous quantities set by initial conditions
- Vary across universes in the ensemble

## Key Hypotheses

1. **Abundance = Frozen History**: Abundance quantities are frozen initial conditions from the early-universe phase transition epoch.

2. **Universe Ensemble**: Different universes with identical identity (topology) have different abundances (history). Our universe is one sample.

3. **Probability Distributions**: Abundance values follow probability distributions determined by the actualization process. Only distributions can be predicted, not exact values.

4. **Common Origin**: All abundance quantities originate from the same epoch (electroweak phase transition) and share a common functional form (scale-invariant or log-normal).

## Mathematical Framework

```
Fix:    Q, Randomness, M² (primitives)
        → Topological identity (particles, forces, dimensions)

Vary:   Initial defect density at EWPT
        → Abundance distribution (masses, couplings, densities)

Predict: P(α), P(m_e), P(Ω_DM), P(M²)
         — probability distributions across the ensemble

Observe: One sample (our universe's values)
```

## Results

- Universe ensemble simulation: 1000 universes with varying abundances
- ~62% are "viable" (atoms + galaxies possible)
- Our universe is inside the viable subset
- No fine-tuning required — ensemble statistics replace anthropic reasoning

## Open Questions

1. Can a unified abundance distribution be derived?
2. Is the distribution scale-invariant (P(ξ) ∝ ξ^(-2))?
3. Can the electroweak scale be predicted as the most probable value?
4. Can the Ω_DM/Ω_b ratio emerge from defect population statistics?
