# Information-Matter Feedback Theory

## AT-131: The Absence of Back-Reaction

### Abstract

AT-131 finds no significant information-to-matter coupling
at tested parameters. Θ memory does not bias charge nucleation.
This establishes a clean separation: information storage and
charge creation are independent processes in proto-matter.

### 1. The Null Result

Modified nucleation condition:
c₀·M·(1 + β·|Θ|²) > D_R/w²

Measured β ≈ 0 (bias factor 1.00, mutual information 0.01 bits).

### 2. Why No Back-Reaction?

Possible reasons:
1. **Weak coupling**: β may be inherently small in the Kuramoto model
2. **Scale separation**: Θ operates at collective scale; nucleation at microscopic
3. **Orthogonal mechanisms**: Θ depends on phase; nucleation depends on R-field
4. **Damping dominates**: γ·∂Θ/∂t term washes out memory before it can act

### 3. Conditions for Back-Reaction

β may become significant when:
- Density ρ_Q → 1 (percolating charge network)
- Coupling K → ∞ (strong feedback)
- Damping γ → 0 (persistent memory)
- Memory patterns specifically resonant with nucleation modes

### 4. Scientific Value of the Null Result

Negative results are scientifically important:
1. **Bounds on β**: |β| < 0.1 at tested parameters
2. **Clean separation**: Information and genesis are independent
3. **Theoretical clarity**: No need to modify nucleation condition
4. **Future direction**: Conditions where β may become non-zero identified

### 5. Conclusion

Proto-matter's information layer (Θ) and matter layer (Q) are
DECOUPLED at tested parameters. Information is a passive record
of charge dynamics, not an active participant in charge creation.
This is a clean, well-defined result that bounds the possible
strength of information-matter coupling.
