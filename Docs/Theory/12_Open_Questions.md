# 12. Open Questions

## Priority-Ordered After TQM-154

---

## Priority 1: Measurement Problem (Irreducible)

**Status**: UNSOLVED since 1926. Affects ALL QM formulations.

**What we know**: Decoherence explains interference disappearance. Pointer states explain stable basis. Born statistics on diagonal.

**What we don't know**: Why ONE outcome occurs. Collapse mechanism. "And" → "or" transition.

**TQM angle**: Can Q-network environment structure provide new insights? Does graph topology affect decoherence rates or pointer state selection?

---

## Priority 2: Experimental Realization

**Status**: No physical experiment proposed or conducted.

**Candidate platforms**: Coupled oscillator arrays, superconducting qubits, cold atoms, photonic waveguides, spin networks.

**Key question**: Can Q-like topological charges be realized and measured?

---

## Priority 3: Dynamic Graph Topology

**Status**: Theoretical possibility, unexplored.

**Hypothesis**: If Q charges can move (changing L_Q(t)), innovation may become open-ended (niche construction).

---

## Priority 4: Higher Dimensions

**Status**: Partially explored (TQM-143 tested 1D-3D).

**Key question**: Do higher dimensions produce qualitatively new phenomena? Topological edge states? Phase transitions?

---

## Priority 5: Nonlinear Q Interactions

**Status**: TQM-141 tested nonlinear mode composition (not essential).

**Key question**: Under what conditions do nonlinearities produce new phenomena (solitons, chaos)?

---

## Priority 6: Continuum Limit

**Status**: Scaling laws derived for large Q. Exact relationship unexplored.

**Key question**: How do species, fitness, and evolution behave as Q → ∞?

---

## Priority 7: Born Rule Derivation

**Status**: Gleason's theorem provides uniqueness. Additivity is the assumption.

**Key question**: Can additivity be derived from Q-network structure?

---

## Priority 8: Thermodynamic Limit

**Status**: Unexplored. Phase transitions? Equation of state?

---

## Priority 9: Information-Theoretic Foundations

**Status**: C = log₂(Q) established. Deeper connections unexplored.

---

## Priority 10: QM Interpretations Comparison

**Status**: Not yet done. Compare with many-worlds, Bohmian, QBism, relational QM.
