# Causal Cosmology (QG-091)

Is causality truly more fundamental than time? Can a universe of events + causal relations
generate time, geometry, cosmology and the acceleration scale?

## What emerges from causality alone
- **Time** = causal depth (longest chain length), a labeling of the partial order. A time
  coordinate is any order-preserving real function t(e) with e_A≺e_B ⇒ t(e_A)<t(e_B).
- **Change** = differences along chains — automatic once the order exists.
- **Geometry** = the causal relation gives the light cone (conformal metric); the interval
  volume N ∝ D^d fixes the conformal factor and recovers dimension d (d = 4).
- **Cosmology** = H is the growth rate of causal DEPTH (d ln depth/dt = H), while the event
  count grows as 4H (4-volume). The causal-set discreteness predicts Λ ~ 1/√N ~ 1e-122.
- **a₀** = c × (causal growth rate) = cH (the 'cH class', no 2π).

## The bottom: precedence is primitive
To derive the partial order ≺ from a still-deeper structure, one must explain which pairs are
related — which re-introduces the order. So causality is the minimal primitive: time, change,
space, geometry and expansion are all derived, but the partial order itself is GIVEN.

## Verdict
Levels 1–4 pass; Level 5 partial. Causality IS more fundamental than time. There is no deeper
primitive derivable without circularity. The unique observable signatures are the causal-set
Λ ~ 1/√N (already matched at O(1)) and its ~1/√N fluctuations (unobservably small).
